# Origin: from social physics to relational authority

## 1. The founding ambition

NOMOS began with a social-physics intuition.

The original project tried to describe a person caught between two kinds of constraint:

- a **physical field**: heat, light, fatigue, distance, bodily limitation, material opportunity;
- a **normative field**: mourning expectations, motives, responsibility, character, legal and social interpretation.

The early ambition was intentionally extreme: could the mismatch between those fields be measured, perhaps even treated like a kind of social-physical stress or singularity?

That ambition mattered. It forced the project to ask whether apparently personal outcomes are partly generated by the surrounding world.

But the literal physics did not survive.

## 2. The first self-destruction

NOMOS-0.1 retained the two layers only after attacking them.

It retired:

- two literal ontological universes;
- normative forces analogous to physical forces;
- actual energy conversion between fields;
- a one-way social translation operator;
- a fixed "absurdity" threshold;
- universal weights and scores;
- any claim that mismatch alone proves a person's essence.

What survived was narrower and stronger:

> material/sensory constraints and normative/institutional interpretation answer different questions, interact bidirectionally, and should not be collapsed.

The first durable methodological move was therefore not a theory of society. It was a **discipline of anti-collapse**.

## 3. The founding explanandum that survived

The durable NOMOS chain is:

**situated event → local interpretation → cross-event linkage → person model → institutional authority → consequence**

The recurring failure is not that interpretation exists. Interpretation is unavoidable.

The failure appears when an institution or community silently changes the type of claim:

- an event becomes evidence about a person without a bridge;
- a local reason becomes a global character;
- a policy-conditioned outcome becomes an intrinsic trait;
- a jointly produced result becomes person-only evidence;
- a record inherits authority after its original warrant has been defeated;
- a support-dependent state is redescribed as personal failure;
- a practical need to act is converted into confidence about who the person is.

NOMOS studies those transitions.

## 4. Why "social physics" still matters

The phrase remains historically useful if it is treated as a **failed generative model**.

Its valid residue is relational:

1. outcomes depend on configurations, not isolated bodies;
2. the environment can constrain what actions are reachable;
3. normative expectations can alter attention, behavior, observation and consequence;
4. institutions can change the evidence they later use;
5. support, barriers and rules can be causally load-bearing;
6. a person-level record can erase the relational system that produced it.

The mature replacement for fields and forces is therefore a typed graph or hypergraph:

**persons + events + supports + environments + institutions + interpretations + claims + provenance + authority**

Edges are typed. They do not all mean "cause." Some enable, constrain, observe, interpret, ratify, transport, authorize, defeat or repair.

## 5. The anti-museum rule

This repository is not a dump of every NOMOS version.

Historical material survives here only when it explains a current invariant.

Examples:

- `0.1` survives because it establishes layer separation and destroys literal physics.
- `0.25` survives because causal responsibility cannot silently become moral or legal responsibility.
- `0.206–0.207` survive because co-production is neither exoneration nor privatization.
- `0.752` survives because event ownership does not itself establish person relevance.
- `0.819` survives because distributed production needs role-indexed accountability and a reassembly route.
- `0.830–0.834` survive because policy, recovery, support and shared causation alter what person-level inference is warranted.

Everything else belongs in the full laboratory archive unless it changes the active kernel.

## 6. Current scientific identity

NOMOS is best described as a research program in **relational person-judgment and institutional epistemic authority**.

It asks:

> when a world containing persons, supports, institutions, records and interpretations produces an outcome, what may legitimately be inferred about the person, by whom, for which consequence, under which provenance and defeat conditions?

That is the surviving form of the original attempt to measure absurdity.
