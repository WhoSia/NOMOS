# P2-P20 — Keep the two SCHUFA decisions distinct

- **Scoring:** C‑634/21, ECLI:EU:C:2023:957, 7 December 2023, paras. 46–51, 60–64 and operative part. Where the recipient draws strongly on a credit agency's automated probability value in a contractual decision, the scoring act can itself fall within Article 22(1). This is case-conditioned decisiveness, not a universal statement that a recipient always acquiesces. [CJEU judgment](https://juris.curia.europa.eu/juris/document/document.jsf?cid=1505002&docid=280426&doclang=EN&mode=lst&occ=first&pageIndex=0&part=1)
- **Discharge-data retention:** Joined C‑26/22 and C‑64/22, ECLI:EU:C:2023:958, same day, operative points 2–4 and paragraphs 94–103. The specified practice of private credit agencies retaining public discharge information beyond the public register's storage period is precluded. This cannot decide whether a later private decision relied on a version and who reopened it. For concurrent storage during the public period, read the separate analysis in paras. 100–103. [CJEU judgment](https://juris.curia.europa.eu/juris/document/document_print.jsf?cid=3526455&docid=280428&doclang=EN&mode=DOC&occ=first&pageIndex=0&part=1&text=)

These texts are strong prior authority about record retention and decisional significance. NOMOS's proposed comparison concerns evidence of the movement from one correction to one identifiable later decision. No transfer of EU legal scope to US housing cases is assumed.
