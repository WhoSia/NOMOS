# NOMOS-0.885 — P2-P0: Claim-Relative Authority Transfer, Adversarial Falsification Program

Date opened: 2026-11-05. **Status: P2 RESEARCH STAGE ADMITTED; P2 RESULT / NOVELTY / REAL OUTCOME NOT VERIFIED.**

## Admission basis and scope
P1-R1–R17 established direct official examples distinguishing a legal order's termination, conditional reporting duties, and unknown individual remedy outcomes. It also created explicit nonidentification counterworlds, a source-actor ledger, and failures of earlier validators. R17's control finds **no decision improvement** over a strong manually curated legal baseline in synthetic fixtures. This negative result is a reason to open a bounded adversarial test, not evidence of originality.

The purpose of P2 is to falsify this concrete conjecture: claim-relative source authority plus lawful receiving-role and intended purpose, when expressed in a transparent audit contract, exposes some materially incorrect institutional conclusion that competent expert baseline B1 misses on independently reviewed real cases. We accept a null result as a valid scientific outcome.

## Preregistration: comparators
- B0: document existence or provenance-path completeness only; intentionally weak diagnostic.
- B1: a strong expert review of statutory triggering conditions, legal authority, purpose, actor roles, scope and dates, as used in the same cases; human-readable and source-cited.
- G: additional typed claim-use contract and explicit unobserved edges, with identical information budget and sources as B1.
- Critical competitor: W3C PROV-O plus domain-specific source validation and known legal purpose restrictions. Do not label established features as novel.
- Outcome: claim-level adjudicated error counts by independent reviewer, abstention calibration, extra review time; no consumer-level welfare estimates unless independently sourced.

## Admission does NOT entail closure
P1 is not closed. P2-P0 starts only methodology registration. Promotion to P2 tested hypothesis is blocked until:
1. Freeze a non-development independent sample with page-level document citations and bounded claim set.
2. Use a review protocol that does not leak target labels or private individual data.
3. Observe a nontrivial B1↔G disagreement; audit adjudication, not raw count alone.
4. Provide a source-backed correct resolution and credible alternative explanation.
5. Read back exact HEAD CI, Drive original bytes and Notion scientific decisions separately.
6. Preserve negative results, failed tests and predecessor paths.

## Non-claims
No new legal doctrine, real recipient notice, complete individual redress, extra causal influence, proprietary person identifiers, novel PROV ontology, or superiority over expert legal auditors is certified.

## P2-P0 candidate primitive
**An item may be genuinely evidenced, yet its transfer into a different institution's authority to classify a person can remain unwarranted even when the original evidence is unchanged.** Existing relevance, limited-purpose rules and NOMOS predecessor 0.838–0.850, 0.881 already support many instances. P2 must find distinct incremental explanatory behavior or merge back into predecessor work.
