## Portland and Philadelphia protect different steps

- **Portland, PCC 30.01.086(D)(8) and linked Permanent Administrative Rule H.** After an appeal approval for a covered application, the applicant has three months of prequalification at the landlord's properties for units at the same or lower rental rate. The rule restricts repeat screening and fees, with possible unchanged-conditions self-certification. For a qualifying vacancy the landlord must notify prequalified applicants before public offering and allow at least 48 hours to declare intent, triggered as specified by the channel; if several respond within the allotted time, the rule orders the offer by appeal submission dates. Thus an approval can generate a later notice and priority, conditional on supply and response. [Code](https://www.portland.gov/code/30/01/086) · [City rule PDF](https://www.portland.gov/phb/documents/hou-306-rental-housing-application-and-screening-permanent-rule/download)
- **Philadelphia, Code §9-810(4)–(5), cross-referencing §9-1108(4).** The specified denial-notice scenario starts a 48-hour window to give notice of dispute or reconsideration; after timely notice the applicant may provide evidence within seven business days after denial. If the original unit is rented to someone else, a landlord owning at least five units offered for rent in the city must offer the next available unit of comparable size and rental price when the application plus submitted information meet the reasonableness test. This is a conditional offer duty, attaching to the displaced applicant and actual comparable supply. Neither jurisdiction is being applied to the historical Connecticut or Massachusetts dispute. [Current code](https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/philadelphia/latest/philadelphia_pa/0-0-0-278115) · [City guide](https://www.phila.gov/documents/renters-access-act-tenant-screening-guidelines/)
- **The voucher is a separate clock.** Under 24 CFR §982.303(c), the housing agency suspends the voucher term when the family submits a request for agency approval of the tenancy, through written notice of the agency's determination. An appeal to a landlord is a distinct event. Extensions under subsection (b), and specific local programs, need separate facts; this is not a claim that Arroyo's RAP subsidy was subject to the federal HCV rule. [Section text](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/24/982.303) · [HUD guidebook, housing search and leasing](https://www.hud.gov/sites/dfiles/PIH/documents/HCV-Guidebook_Housing-Search-and-Leasing-Chapter_June-2025.pdf)

The columns identify different duties, not equivalent outcomes. When a landlord has no qualifying vacancy, even a favorable decision may not yield an offer. When many people qualify, priority and applicant response become part of the causal chain. A municipal layered comparison must observe vacancy events, eligibility/scope, notification timestamp and channel, an offer/response, and whether the opportunity could still be used under the person's subsidy and other deadlines. Prequalification, timely notification, concrete offer and lease are separate endpoints; use a time-to-event analysis with administrative censoring and competing exits only if suitable field data become available.

## Independent evaluation gives an empirical floor and an explicit gap

Bell and Goldstein's September 2024 Reinvestment Fund evaluation of Philadelphia includes 20 interviews with approximately 30 individuals and 80 attempted scripted tests, 70 completed. Table 5 gives 36 compliant, 22 inconclusive and 12 noncompliant in the coded completed tests (counts reconstructed from N=70 and rounded percentages). Phone inquiries did not involve completed applications, so this sample cannot show rates for actual denials, notices, next-unit offers, or leases. Sites and scenarios were selected; the reported 17.1% is not a citywide noncompliance estimate. The report cites nine cases received and processed or being processed by PCHR as of August 14, 2024, including three then open; that is not a population denominator. §9-810(9) itself calls for periodic independent evaluation. NOMOS should build from this fieldwork and add an applicant-level follow-through protocol rather than assert that remedies have never been evaluated. [Evaluation report, pp. 1–2, 7–8, 11–12 and Table 5](https://www.reinvestment.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Reinvestment-Fund-RAA-Report.pdf)

First useful evidence packet: dated favorable review, verified unit availability/assignment record, and issued notice or offer for the same case; add lawful scope and expiry. If notification records are present, an expired voucher or a reporting artifact is not the same as unreachability of reconsideration.
