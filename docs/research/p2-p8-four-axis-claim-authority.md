# NOMOS-0.885 P2-P8 — Four-Dimensional Claim Warrant and Outcome-Scope Audit

Research date: 2026-11-10. Outcome: synthetic model and methodology guard, NOT a real legal verdict or scientific novelty certification.

## Central insight grounded in four new primary works
Park/Nguyen/Sandhu (2012) already control provenance-dependent actions. Sultan (2022) PhD already supplies a role-, content-, context- and purpose-aware implemented access-control prototype. Martin/Nissenbaum/Shmatikov (2025) already report empirical vignette sensitivity to use purpose, including inference/raw-data scenarios. Engelmann/Nissenbaum (2025) already identify **conceptual overfitting** from inappropriate data, construct labels and validation methods. These defeat novelty in a generic 'provenance+purpose+inference+role' stack.

## Proposed four-part contract
For a claim about a data subject, inspect separately:
- E: documentary evidence for the exact event/proposition (a source may be legitimate yet narrow).
- K: epistemic construct validity of the inferred trait: fit between available measurements, target concept and evaluation design.
- A: authority of a particular institution to **assert/use** the claim under controlling rule, purpose and scope.
- O: independent evidence of an actual downstream outcome/consequence; not inferred from issuance of an order or mere availability of data.

Each value is one of `yes`, `no`, `unknown`. **Unknown is never 'no' by default** and no part of a four-coordinate vector can be silently copied to another. This is a method specification; not an assertion that law mandates four predicates in every institution. Some factual claims do not involve a constructed trait and must be handled with a separate rule instead of fabricated K eligibility.

## Explicit false conclusions to prevent
1. E=yes alone does not prove K or A or O.
2. E=K=yes does not establish institutional claim use authority A.
3. E=K=A=yes does not establish observed downstream outcome O.
4. O=yes is a *different empirical claim*, requiring same case/unit authenticated proof, not a permit for unrelated inferences.
5. Negative source evidence (no actual authority in a controlled hypothetical) and *missing* authority information yield different output states.

## Method
Exhaustively enumerate `3^4 = 81` synthetic predicate vectors and assert the derived authority/welfare predicates never promote unknown or no to yes. A consumer-specific real-world verdict requires sourced legal rule, time, actor, scope, claimant and evidence of outcome. No such complete chain is in this unit. Model output is a transparent bookkeeping instrument, not a proven improvement over the strong expert-audit baseline, contextual integrity or the Sultan thesis.

## Future empiricism
Replicate a bounded published vignette (with its measurement instrument, sample and regression specification) or conduct independent blinded institutional-document coding under controlled access. Only then consider effect sizes, adjudicated error rates or added-value claims; the currently available journal text gives published author-reported results but underlying subject-level dataset was not obtained or reanalyzed.

## Stop condition
If B1 expert review and extended contextual-integrity methods implement the same E/K/A/O distinction, NOMOS must not claim a new theory. P2 research ongoing; originality and actual recipient-level outcomes HOLD.


### P2-P8.6 — Four axes must not be collapsed
Park's graph-derived access permissions are not a substitute for epistemic construct validity K. Engelmann/Nissenbaum directly problematize construct labels and ground truth. Conversely, a high-quality inference or justified K does not establish the institution's legal authority A, and no combination of K/A proves that a downstream outcome O occurred. Treat each as a distinct evidence question.


### P2-P8.7 — Competitor strength and inherited method
Martin/Nissenbaum/Shmatikov present real factorial-vignette evidence that contextual purpose matters for perceptions; Sultan's doctoral prototype uses actual XACML/MySQL metadata-based control. The E/K/A/O notation is a conceptual checklist that a competent legal reviewer and extended contextual-integrity rules can already express. Adding four labels is NOT a new formal theorem.


### P2-P8.8 — Closed-world exceptions and nulls
The synthetic model's 'no' means a negative predicate is explicitly stipulated, whereas 'unknown' means no sufficient case record. In real records, absence of a remedy completion certificate must never become empirical noncompliance. In addition, straightforward claims of public legal acts need not instantiate a psychological latent trait K; a domain-specific type system should disable or reinterpret K rather than force a made-up construct warrant.


### P2-P8.9 — Replication target
The next concrete test should target one published survey's factor coding, vignette instrument, sample construction and regression table from Martin et al. and attempt to replicate only with disclosed data; until respondent-level data is found, label 'author-reported findings' and avoid synthesizing effect sizes. In parallel, extract one small XACML policy example from Sultan's dissertation and reproduce it fairly in a baseline engine, not a weakened caricature.
