## Do not substitute operational influence for legal causation or person authority

On pp. 29–34 the Second Circuit rejected a categorical FHA threshold for screening vendors, then held that the challenged vendor practice was not shown to proximately cause the denial on this record. The housing provider selected the criteria, configuration, access to reports, follow-up and final disposition. The court also reversed the district court's FCRA liability ruling concerning the request for disclosure. Under a different question and body of law, [C-634/21, SCHUFA](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:62021CJ0634), ¶¶43–50 and its operative part, turns on a recipient drawing strongly on a probability value. The two judgments are a strong rival for any model that treats formal decision ownership or reported reliance as dispositive across legal regimes.

The original NOMOS question returns at the specific point where an incident becomes a screening consequence: a person-related record can have documentary support and the receiving institution may be able to act under some rule, while a portable character judgment still requires its own justification. Recovery can likewise follow a route that never changed the source record. The [constitution](../constitution.md) already separates action authority, person-predicate warrant and repair responsibility (C2, C5, C17, C22). This case exercises those distinctions and adds source resolution; it does not establish their novelty or empirical superiority.
