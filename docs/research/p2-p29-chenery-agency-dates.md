# P2-P29 — The agency order has its own chronology

The Commission approved the amended Federal Water plan in its September 24, 1941 session. The order was filed with the Federal Register on September 26 and published September 27. Its operative text grants the applications and permits the declarations to take effect subject to five conditions. The order refers separately to findings and an opinion and to supplemental findings and an opinion. [6 FR 4926–4927, FR Doc. 41-7209](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-1941-09-27/pdf/FR-1941-09-27.pdf#page=14).

The remand order records the Commission's February 7, 1945 session. It denies the proposed amendment and reapproves the existing plan and its transactions as of September 24, 1941. The printed order was filed on February 9 and published February 10. The retroactive approval date describes the plan's legal status. [10 FR 1714, FR Doc. 45-2319](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-1945-02-10/pdf/FR-1945-02-10.pdf#page=24).

The [SEC's tenth annual report, p. 88 n. 68](https://www.sec.gov/files/1944.pdf#page=109) reports issuance of the findings and opinion on February 8 under Release 5584. The [printed U.S. Reports account at 332 U.S. 199](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USREPORTS-332/pdf/USREPORTS-332-194.pdf#page=6) also gives February 8. The Federal Register supplies the date on the operative order and keeps the filing date visible. The order refers to findings and an opinion as issued that day. The independent text and release history of the findings are needed to explain the remaining date difference.

*In re Federal Water & Gas Corp.*, [188 F.2d 100 nn. 3, 6](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/F2/188/100/65778/), identifies the reported records as 10 S.E.C. 200 and 18 S.E.C. 231. The [1947 follow-up notice](p2-p32-federal-water-notice.md) separately orders a hearing on a later plan.
