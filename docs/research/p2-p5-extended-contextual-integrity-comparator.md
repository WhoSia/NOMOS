# NOMOS-0.885 P2-P5 — Extended Contextual Integrity Comparator
Research date: 2026-11-10. Status: synthetic adversarial study; scientific novelty and real case results HOLD.

## Question
Is the P2-P4 difference between a permitted flow, a derived attribute and later institutional person-level judgment a formally unique NOMOS contribution? Try to refute it by extending the Barth/Datta/Mitchell/Nissenbaum (2006) event alphabet with an explicit judgment action.

## Method, strict scope
Compare (A) a bounded, **researcher-defined** 'extended contextual trace' monitor enforcing an additional negative norm `judge → previously send and derive AND appropriate current authority predicates` with (B) a separate NOMOS claim-use contract implementation listing unmet predicates. Both use exactly the same six Boolean facts: flow permission, derivation, qualified actor, permitted purpose, scope-supported claim, available contest route. Historical event sequences supply separate send, derive and judge occurrences.

Enumerate 2^6 Boolean conditions × 5 synthetic action sequences = 320 determinate tests. The experiment is deliberately constructed so the two approaches should coincide. It is a capability counterexample to weak novelty: **NOMOS's toy distinction can be reexpressed by a plausible extension of contextual transmission norms.** It is NOT a proof about the full expressive equivalence, complexity or exact semantics of the original Barth formalism, and NOT evidence of actual legality or person-level welfare.

## Primary paper
Barth, Datta, Mitchell & Nissenbaum (2006), Privacy and Contextual Integrity: Framework and Applications, §3.1 inferred-attribute computation; §3.2–3.4 contextual roles and first-order LTL norms; §4.3 strong/weak compliance. ## Threats to validity
Source facts are externally asserted booleans; no court judgment or authentic authority receipt is checked. All-purpose 'contest_enabled' may not be a legal precondition to all classifications. A literal extension adds a new action type and legal predicates that the original 2006 paper did not itself claim to implement. Baseline gets equivalent extra legal facts by design; this is a stress test against false novelty, not a measured superiority study.

## Research decision
If all combinations agree, do not claim an autonomous NOMOS operator was discovered. Only novel externally justified law/fact mechanisms, outcomes, or measurable workflow benefit against an equal-input strong baseline could advance the P2 theorem claim. ### P2-P5.7 — Metamorphic perturbations
The regression asserts that (i) `judge` preceding `send` is rejected, (ii) `judge` after `send` but before `derive` has a missing prior derivation, and (iii) replacing a known positive contestability flag with null blocks a positive modeled warrant. The study does not infer that any specific real institution is legally obliged to provide identical contestability procedures in every context.
