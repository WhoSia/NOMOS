# P2-P34 — Evidence for the receiving decision

[Ibrahim](p2-p33-ibrahim-findings.md) supplies an adjudicated originating error, an order addressing propagation, [sworn accounts of implementation and a dated visa refusal](p2-p33-ibrahim-compliance.md). [Patrick](p2-p32-patrick-opinions-readback.md) supplies the locator for a lender's adverse reason, an earlier reporting-agency response and a reported upstream correction. Both have evidence on the receiving side and open questions about which versions reached which decisionmakers.

| Case anchor | Record-side result | Receiving-side question |
|---|---|---|
| Ibrahim | Early list removal, cross-system correction order and agency implementation reports | Which specific changes preceded the April 14 visa refusal, which version did the consular decisionmaker see, and what did the decisionmaker actually rely on? List status and visa eligibility concern distinct institutional acts. |
| Patrick | The reported corrected furnishing follows the dispute response | Which version reached the mortgage actor, and did the actor reconsider for a stated reason? |
| Kashem | Final decision authority, recommendation and partial reasons can be distinguished in the revised pathway | What case-specific material can the requester or the court examine, and does it establish a particular acknowledged error? |
| Fikre | Status change and an identified communication chain | Who signed the operative decision, and which facts or authority supported it? |

For a paired-reason comparison, identify two authentic acts, the deciding authority, a common decision question, ordered dates and disclosed reasons. Require version-specific evidence separately before attributing reliance on corrected material. Existing credit law, administrative law and ordinary record audit remain the comparison baseline under identical source access. A dated refusal supplies a real receiving decision. A case can supply such a decision while leaving a matched before-and-after comparison and the effect of correction open.
