# P2-P21 — Cross-domain source check: CoreLogic and Services Australia

The one-page [Services Australia release LEX 90226](https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/sites/default/files/2026-03/lex-90226-document.pdf) gives RIN0319 an update date of 13 November 2025. Its overview reports roughly 530,630 historical debt records touching 434,895 customers. It identifies 184,357 Non-Averaged Only debt records for the 2020–21 initial activity that zeroed those records and states that this activity was completed. The document describes phase two as developing remediation options. It also reports ad hoc remediation where affected debt records have been modified. The text says there is no impact on current payments while retaining the possibility of historical underpayment or overpayment.

| Source | What is visible | What would discriminate between the surviving explanations |
|---|---|---|
| CoreLogic, appeal and JA-586–592 | Recipient-specific reversal plus a later signed agreement. The June permission and August signatures are distinct dated events. | A June decision notice or reasons record identifying the materials the manager actually used, a versioned report if one was transmitted, and an independently documented occupancy endpoint. |
| Services Australia, LEX 90226 / RIN0319 | Completed first-phase activity on a defined administrative record subset, plus an account of later planning and ad hoc work. The unit counted is a debt record. | One matched record/history showing its before/after state, the decision that reused it, the authority and reason code, and any resulting notice, liability or payment change. |

Housing has a recipient action without an identified revised-input lineage. RIN0319 has a documented record-side intervention without a matched person-level downstream action. Both missing links are visible to a strong source-aware adjudicator. No separation from that baseline has been demonstrated. For the next comparison, give both reviewers the same matched trace and require judgments on a bounded consequence and a distinct person-directed claim. The missing link must remain unknown whenever that trace does not address it. Do not infer that zeroing a debt reconstructs a person's historical income or establishes any person trait.
