# NOMOS-0.885 P2-P16 — Who bears the work of making a correction effective?

2026-11-14. Continuation of P2-P15. Public-record close reading, theory proposal and constructed counterexamples.

## CoreLogic: the route from a blocked application to a usable benefit

The [joint appendix, vol. 1](https://www.nhlp.org/wp-content/uploads/Appx-1.pdf) changes the case packet in three respects.

- JA-205–206, stipulated facts 24–32: the vendor generated a personalized adverse-action template directing disclosure to the vendor and disclaiming its own decision role. WinnResidential controlled dispatch of the letter. This identifies who produced the template and who could send it, not a receipt. The parties stipulated that the charge was withdrawn on April 20, 2017; that Arroyo remained in the nursing home until June 2017; and that when he was permitted to be added to the lease, the household's rent share was reduced to 30% of adjusted income under RAP. This last fact is evidence of a concrete financial consequence beyond the reversal. It does not measure how long screening caused the nursing-home stay or establish the move-in date.

In [JA-684–685](https://www.nhlp.org/wp-content/uploads/Appx-4.pdf) he associated permission with CHRO, did not believe a replacement CrimSAFE report was ordered and acknowledged limited knowledge of conversations above him.

## Prior work and competing explanations

[Moynihan, Herd & Harvey, JPART 25:43–69](https://doi.org/10.1093/jopart/muu009) distinguish learning, psychological and compliance costs and ask who bears them. [Lageson, Adams & Chen, LSR 60:387–413](https://doi.org/10.1017/lsr.2026.10094), interviews with 105 eligible people across CA/NJ/PA/UT, already finds that automated initiation can shift work to the state while missing notifications, partial clearances and opaque systems impose new burdens. This preempts any novelty claim that automation shifts burdens or that formal expungement can be hard to use. Their interviews support mechanisms and reported experience, not population incidence or causal effects.

[Lageson & Stewart, Criminology 62:5–34](https://doi.org/10.1111/1745-9125.12359), §4.1.1/Table 3 and limitations: 101 people recruited through legal aid and workforce networks; one regulated vendor and one unregulated source. Missing dispositions accounted for 9.3% and 23% of charges in the respective private reports, not percentages of all applicants. The state comparator also had missing dispositions. Participant 76 reported abandoning a petition after an old arrest appeared unresolved in a state record. The case shows a potentially self-blocking process: defects can discourage entry into the procedure that could lead to relief. A completion-only audit omits that denominator. No population estimate follows.

[Agan et al., 2024 NBER WP 32394](https://www.nber.org/papers/w32394), introduction and results, finds no detectable average employment improvement in the studied settings and a gig-platform exception; the [2026 journal landing page](https://doi.org/10.1257/pol.20240830) confirms the broad result. Against [Prescott & Starr's Michigan petition-based study](https://repository.law.umich.edu/mlr/vol118/iss7/3/), populations, selection and treatments differ. A clean record may be fully propagated while past unemployment, prior decisions or missed opportunities remain. Labor-market scarring is a competing explanation, not a substitute for transmission evidence or a separately identified mechanism here.

## The injustice and a falsifiable research question

An institution can act on a short vendor output while the affected household must locate the original disposition, learn who can revise which decision, establish standing or authority, and identify which institutions already relied on the record. When those institutions have the report versions, routing logs and decision reasons, routing the burden to the least informed actor is a design choice. That is a normative assessment of burden allocation, not a determination of illegality or of anyone's intent. A later reversal can be fully effective for one household while the same erroneous basis remains reusable elsewhere. Conversely all known copies can be updated while a closed opportunity remains lost.

The law already allocates some of this work. [15 USC §1681i(a)(2), (d)](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-124/t15/s1681i/) distinguishes notifying the furnisher of a dispute from notifying eligible prior report recipients after specified deletion/notation, on consumer request and specific designation, with lookbacks of two years for employment and six months for other purposes. [GDPR Article 19](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng) instead requires the controller to communicate qualifying changes to recipients subject to impossibility/disproportionate effort, and to identify recipients to the subject on request. These are bounded communication rules; neither cited paragraph establishes what a recipient later did. The comparison is structural, with no cross-jurisdiction applicability claim. In SCHUFA, [C-634/21 ¶¶48–50 and operative ruling](https://juris.curia.europa.eu/juris/document/document.jsf?docid=280426&doclang=EN) addresses when a score is itself an automated decision because of the recipient's reliance. [C-26/22 and C-64/22, operative ruling 2](https://juris.curia.europa.eu/juris/document/document_print.jsf?docid=280428&doclang=EN) addresses impermissible extended private retention of public debt-discharge data in that setting. Neither establishes that a particular lender reconsidered.

**Candidate test:** start from everyone exposed to the original decision input, including non-complainants. Compare actual workflows by who detects the discrepancy, who can identify prior recipients, who can certify reclassification, what the decision deadline is and who can reopen it. Measure independently dated version receipt, decision reason and durable benefit. Observed resolution rate among completed complaints is a separate outcome. Consider exceptions for missing logs, sensitive disclosures and legitimate independent grounds for refusal. Give the identical packet to the strong legal, contextual-integrity and relational baseline plus NOMOS. If experts already identify the same reopening failures and evidence needs, the proposed framework earns no comparative advantage.

Two constructed checks: (A) corrected source and acknowledged receipt, but no reopened application before its deadline. (B) a successful individual appeal while the old result is still available for reuse in a separate transaction. These show why neither receipt nor a favorable decision is a sufficient proxy for both individual repair and recurring-use prevention. The cases are conceptual controls and no independent benchmark has been run.
