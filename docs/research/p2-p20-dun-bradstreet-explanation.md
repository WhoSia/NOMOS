# P2-P20 — Dun & Bradstreet Austria and the object of explanation

**C‑203/22, ECLI:EU:C:2025:117, 27 February 2025.** The case concerns a credit assessor and the enforcement of a disclosure order. Article 15(1)(h), as applied to a decision within Article 22(1), requires an intelligible account of the procedure and principles actually used to obtain the specific result. Read the operative part alongside paragraphs 43–62. Paragraph 62 gives data variation as one way a national court could find the explanation sufficiently transparent in a profiling case. It does not mandate a particular counterfactual format or publish-all model disclosure.

Paragraphs 67–74 and point 2 of the ruling place allegedly protected information before the competent supervisory authority or court, which balances interests and decides the extent of access. Route it to that decision-maker without assuming the material either must be public or may be categorically withheld. This answers an access question about case-specific scoring. To show that a separately acting recipient then received, used, or acted on a correction, seek evidence of the later recipient and decision.
