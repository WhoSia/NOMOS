# NOMOS-0.885 P2-P18 — Reopening a decision and recovering a usable opportunity

2026-11-18. Follow-up to P2-P16 on responsibility for reopening and P2-P17 on alteration of a report versus use of that report.

## Three source-backed distinctions

- **Louis and Douglas / SafeRent.** Amended complaint, ECF 15 ¶¶75–92 and the 2023 motion-to-dismiss order ECF 64, pp. 5–6, 18–19. The pleading says Metropolitan told Louis it did not accept appeals and could not override the screening outcome. That is the intake channel the landlord represented to the applicant. It does not establish a product-wide technical lock, the landlord's actual internal permissions, or legal authority to refuse review. The same pleading describes Douglas's personal appeal being rejected and another submission with City Life/Vida Urbana reaching Corcoran in early September 2021, followed by acceptance. The order summarizes these as pleadings, not trial findings. The cases involved different landlords and applicants. The pleading ¶87 names Metropolitan for the first appeal response while ¶¶85 and 88–89 locate Douglas's case at Corcoran. Avoid assigning the first response to a specific entity without the cited letters. Neither document proves a score update, a causal effect of advocacy, or a post-settlement implementation event. [Amended complaint](https://clearinghouse-umich-production.s3.amazonaws.com/media/doc/160026.pdf) · [ECF 64](https://www.justice.gov/d9/2023-08/memo_saferent-final.pdf)
- **Arroyo / CoreLogic.** District findings 58–60 and damages discussion, ECF 317 pp. 56–58. Earlier disclosure would not establish earlier housing by itself. The court noted the record did not establish suitable two-bedroom availability, whether early release from the existing lease would be allowed, or why WinnResidential ultimately authorized the application. The court could infer that the CHRO action was at least a cause, without establishing when an earlier application would have succeeded. The joint stipulation in JA-206 reports a realized rent-share consequence when the lease addition was allowed. That observation leaves the earlier counterfactual unresolved. Read alongside, and do not displace, the later appellate disposition of the claims. [District decision](https://www.nhlp.org/wp-content/uploads/Arroyo-Decision.pdf) · [Joint appendix vol. 1](https://www.nhlp.org/wp-content/uploads/Appx-1.pdf) · [Appeal](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCOURTS-ca2-23-01166/pdf/USCOURTS-ca2-23-01166-0.pdf)
- **Portland, Oregon.** PCC 30.01.086(D)(8) provides a 30-day appeal opportunity for covered applications. It allows correction, refutation or explanation. Following approval on appeal the applicant is prequalified at the same landlord's Portland properties for three months and receives the screening-fee waiver provided by the code, with a possible unchanged-conditions self-certification before the waiver. This is a concrete example of relief carrying forward to later vacancies in a bounded fleet of properties. It does not reserve the original unit, guarantee another vacancy or establish enforcement or take-up. Exceptions in subsection G matter. This Oregon rule is a comparator for institutional design and has not been applied to the Massachusetts or Connecticut cases. [Official code](https://www.portland.gov/code/30/01/086) · [City application and screening materials](https://www.portland.gov/phb/rental-services/application-and-screening)

## Measures for a matched-case pilot

Freeze the cohort at the initial adverse decision. For a fixed review route and observation window use nested events O (reopened for substantive review), F (decision favorable on that review) and U (at least one usable opportunity attributable to that favorable decision, by a declared criterion such as an actual available unit or a documented prequalification used at another eligible property). Use claim-relevant timestamps for notice, appeal, meaningful review, approval and expiration. For this route P(U) = P(O) P(F|O) P(U|F), if conditioning denominators exist. The identity is descriptive, not causal. Separate routes are needed for compensation, alternate housing acquired independently and preventive changes for future applicants. Track missing records as missing, and never infer a real housing offer from prequalification alone.

| Constructed setting | Initially denied | Reopened | Favorable | Usable | Favorable / reopened | Usable / initially denied |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Earlier decision | 100 | 80 | 40 | 36 | 50% | 36% |
| Access blocked | 100 | 20 | 10 | 9 | 50% | 9% |
| Decision too late | 100 | 80 | 40 | 9 | 50% | 9% |

Arithmetic check: three settings have a 50% reversal rate among reopened cases; their usable-opportunity rates are 36%, 9% and 9%. The second and third share an end rate despite different bottlenecks. The remedy target differs: substantively reachable review in one, ability to use a successful review in the other. These are hypothetical counts, no measured effect or distribution. For actual evaluation observe coverage of all eligible refusals (including people who never appeal), compare like properties and vintages, and avoid attributing group differences to the process without addressing selection, resource availability and remaining lawful decision grounds.

## Prior art and open evidence

[Binns and Veale (2021), pp. 329–331](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10132775/8/Binns_Veale_Is%20that%20your%20final%20decision.pdf) already show how upstream automation can foreclose downstream options despite a final human step. [Green (2022)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.clsr.2022.105681) gives a separate challenge to generic human-oversight requirements. Portland supplies an implemented legal design on paper for limited carryover, not novel NOMOS policy. The testable NOMOS question is whether equal-information coding can distinguish unavailable review from review arriving after the opportunity closes using issuer evidence, recipient evidence, and the case-specific clocks. Compare against legal/contextual/relational baselines without making an originality or superiority claim. Missing: actual authority and routing for each reviewer, original appeal/approval correspondence, person-level benefit where unspecified, SafeRent §3.5 implementation evidence. Public dockets and settlement documents checked this round still did not establish those missing facts.
