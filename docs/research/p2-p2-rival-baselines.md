## Null hypothesis
After all strong rivals are allowed to encode context, source, actor, purpose, normative transmission and claim boundaries, NOMOS adds no novel adjudicated correct conclusions. Accept this result as a successful falsification, rather than inflate novelty.

## Proposed incremental question
A permitted information flow and an authorized receiving role may not justify a broader person-classifying proposition; require explicit inference grounds and person-specific contestation where applicable. But a trained legal auditor and contextual integrity might already capture that. We need a real discordance, independent adjudication, and a decision-useful observable; synthetic agreement/difference cannot establish empirical superiority.

## Evaluation protocol
Case-level and claim-level unit of analysis; preserve case id, underlying doctrine and court date. Blind annotators to study hypothesis and rival outputs. Encode adjudication labels as SUPPORTED, CONTRADICTED, NOT_ESTABLISHED and OUT_OF_SCOPE with passage locators. Compare strong B1 and G on same source packet and time budget. Metrics only if independent gold judgments obtained: paired adjudicated errors, abstention, adjudicator uncertainty, completion time. Otherwise report no measured performance.

### P2-P2.6 — Major novelty defeater
The first novelty firewall is NOT merely W3C PROV-O. Nissenbaum (2004) explicitly centers context-dependent appropriateness and distribution of personal information. Barth et al. (2006) provide formal temporal logic for norms constraining transmission histories. Sultan/Jensen (2021) bind purpose metadata to provenance-enabled access enforcement. Together these directly rival NOMOS's 'actor/context/purpose/transmission/provenance' formula; treating it as novel is inaccurate.


### P2-P2.7 — FOIA domain-specific distinction
For DOJ v Reporters Committee, an unsophisticated 'purpose-based disclosure' code could incorrectly switch ruling based on requester's claimed motive. The Court instead focuses on the FOIA's own central accountability purpose and privacy interests in compiled criminal records under exemption 7(C). This example demonstrates that a generalized policy model requires controlling-domain law, rather than a context-free purpose flag.


### P2-P2.8 — Legitimate authority positive control
NASA v Nelson is a positive control for lawful and functionally justified government collection with Privacy Act safeguards; an algorithm that concludes government collection inherently equals an unauthorized person-judgment transfer is unsound. Preserve the Supreme Court's assumption-without-decision on constitutional right.
