## Research question
Does evidence custody or authorized flow entail authority for a *specific person-classifying proposition*? Do not confuse access, dissemination, contextual permissible use, legal standing and causally observed consequences.

## Primary legal cases
1. DOJ v. Reporters Committee for Freedom of the Press, 489 U.S. 749 (1989). FBI rap sheets compiled from publicly occurring records can invoke FOIA Exemption 7(C) privacy; FOIA central purpose is scrutiny of agency conduct, NOT the requester's own use purpose. The Court's FOIA requester identity rule is crucial negative guard. https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/489/749
2. NASA v. Nelson, 562 U.S. 134 (2011). Employment background questions under federal contractor program did not violate assumed informational privacy right; Court relied on employment interests and Privacy Act dissemination safeguards. This is a **legitimate collection** counterexample, not a categorical prohibition. https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/562/134/
3. TransUnion LLC v. Ramirez, 594 U.S. 413 (2021). Third-party dissemination for 1853 with misleading alerts versus no third-party disclosure for 6332 during class period mattered for Article III concrete harm in federal damages action; not generic proof that every internal error is legally harmless, nor all individuals achieved redress. https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/20-297
