# P2-P33 — Ibrahim's filed correction declaration and the remaining outcome question

[Public reproduction of ECF 736, 737 and Exs. 1–8](https://papersplease.org/wp/wp-content/uploads/2014/04/ibrahim-declarations-15apr2014.pdf), N.D. Cal. No. 3:06-cv-00545-WHA, April 15, 2014.

ECF 736 p. 2 tells the court the defendants have complied and points to a separate declaration. ECF 737 pp. 2–3 gives a sworn account by government counsel. Paragraphs 2–6 identify certifications filed by five officials for the FBI, TSC, DHS, State Department and NCTC (Exs. 1–5). These are identified official certifications of the §A search-and-correction efforts. This reading verified the readable declaration and exhibit cover locators; the individual image-only declaration bodies have not been verified page by page. Thus the exact entry changed, the method selected for each record, and any independent post-change read-back are not established in this audit.

ECF 737 ¶¶7–8 and Ex. 7 identify a separate April 15 counsel-to-counsel transmission of the State Department declaration on older visa grounds, a letter on eligibility to apply for a visa waiver, and the FBI's No Fly List status letter. Counsel asks the recipient attorney to forward the two attachments to her client. This demonstrates the dated dispatch to counsel and the stated purpose. It does not establish final personal receipt, visa adjudication or recipient use of any corrected record. Use the source-specific location ECF 737-6 for the waiver/visa materials and ECF 737-8 for the FBI status letter. The sponsor's secondary description is available [here](https://papersplease.org/wp/2014/04/16/fbi-tells-dr-ibrahim-shes-not-on-the-no-fly-list/).

The case now supports three propositions with different bases: trial-adjudicated error, an enforceable correction order, and sworn reported implementation. An externally verified downstream decision or independent per-record read-back is still open. [Merits order and remedy](p2-p33-ibrahim-findings.md).
