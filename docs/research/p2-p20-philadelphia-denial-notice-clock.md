# P2-P20 — The Philadelphia denial notice clock

Read Philadelphia §§9-1108(4) and 9-810(4)–(5) together. Section 9-1108(4), subject to its stated exceptions and the FCRA limitation on third-party material, calls for written or electronic reasons and relevant third-party copies within three business days of rejection. Under §9-810(4), in the specified notice scenario the applicant has 48 hours after receiving denial to convey an intent to dispute and, on timely notice, seven business days after the denial to provide evidence. Keep rejection, notice delivery and receipt separately timestamped, and preserve the literal texts of both deadline anchors. Avoid automatically starting either timer from internal score generation.

For §9-810(5), a next comparable unit depends on the original unit being rented to another applicant, portfolio and qualification conditions, and a later comparable vacancy. Notice issuance, actual receipt, review, vacancy offer and lease require different evidence. This row can be tested against an access-only packet which contains a successful disclosure but no sent, received or actionable housing offer.
