# P2-P27 — Direction review from person attribution to institutional reuse

The original NOMOS question asks when a judgment of an act, statement or situated refusal becomes an institutionally authorized judgment about the person. Prior versions already distinguish authority to act, draw an inference and reuse a person predicate downstream. Keep housing screening as an application and institutional reuse of a person-level conclusion as the common research object. [P2-P24 origin audit](p2-p24-origin-check.md) · [0.883](../../spec/representational-standing-883.md) · [0.885](../../spec/purpose-bound-record-afterlives-885.md).

| Observed contrast | Closest comparator | Next observable test |
|---|---|---|
| A concrete instruction excludes character information in a case simulation, and estimates move toward each other across positive and negative frames | [Ko, Study 3-2](p2-p25-ko-study-3.md) | A known fact whose downstream use is expressly outside the new decision's authorized scope |
| Earlier feedback continues to affect some self- and other-ratings after being discredited | [Ross et al., Experiments 1–2](p2-p26-ross-debriefing.md) | A documented incorrect source and the named decisionmaker's actual use of a descendant after receiving its correction |
| A disposition or its legal grounds change after review | [Fikre](p2-p27-fikre-record.md) · [Chenery and Regents](p2-p23-administrative-reasons.md) | A matched official reason pair and a dated record tying both to the same corrected fact |
| One context is used to justify a person-wide portrait | [Camus and Le Hir](p2-p24-origin-check.md) | A blinded annotation identifies what establishes case relevance and what supports a claim in the receiving task |

Compare the proposed method to a joint legal and ordinary provenance review with identical case packets. Give that comparator the same authorities, dates, recipients, reasons and record versions. Primary endpoint is each reviewer's support status for a particular downstream person predicate, with cited evidence. Secondary endpoint is the additional document that could reverse each status. Include a reaffirmed decision supported by an independent ground, a derivative-reuse case and a case with undisclosed reasons. Use an independent reviewer to resolve disagreements under an agreed codebook without deriving the labels from the new tool.

Advancement requires at least one positive authenticated packet with a person-specific factual correction, acknowledgement by the relevant authority, earlier and later reasons and identifiable versions. Count Fikre only as a negative control for completeness. If equal-information reviews converge with no additional observational leverage, present this as a synthesis and reproducibility contribution. Keep Camus in the motivating analysis, scoped to the narrator's, prosecutor's and witnesses' statements.
