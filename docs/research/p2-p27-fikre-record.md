# P2-P27 — An administrative disposition can reverse before its factual basis is reviewable

This is a nonhousing negative control, tracing official records around one person's U.S. No Fly listing. The [certiorari petition's original agency exhibits, appendices F–H](https://www.supremecourt.gov/DocketPDF/22/22-1178/268295/20230602180319346_FBI%20v.%20Fikre%20Final.pdf#page=150), [2024 U.S. Reports opinion](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/23pdf/601us1r11_7lh8.pdf), and [2025 District of Oregon order](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCOURTS-ord-3_13-cv-00899/pdf/USCOURTS-ord-3_13-cv-00899-8.pdf) are separate sources.

| Record | What the public record establishes | Evidence gap |
|---|---|---|
| DHS TRIP, 23 Jan 2014, App. H 122a–123a | Initial administrative response says the agencies reviewed applicable records and found no change warranted | The letter does not identify the listing evidence or a contested fact |
| TSA final determination, 9 Mar 2015, App. G 119a–121a | TSA acknowledges the claimant's denial and states a security criterion and a maintain outcome. The decision expressly withholds portions of its basis | Acknowledgement of a contention does not establish acceptance, and the criterion does not identify a source version |
| TSC status change communicated 6 May 2016, D. Or. ECF 98 and App. F ¶4 | Delisting occurs and counsel receives notice | The Supreme Court reports that no explanation accompanied the notice. The original ECF 98 PDF and any separate internal decision basis have not been inspected |
| TSC sworn declaration, 19 Jun 2019, App. F 117a–118a | Declarant says placement and removal followed applicable processes and gives a future assurance bounded to information currently available | A litigation representation about future listing does not retrospectively supply the reason for delisting or a version of the corrected record |
| *FBI v. Fikre*, 601 U.S. 234, 242–245 (2024) | The government had not carried its burden to show mootness on the record and posture presented | The Court left the allegations to be tested and distinguished proof about future conduct from formal repudiation |

Do not treat standing, mootness, an allegation the Court temporarily accepts for analysis, the listing criterion, and validity of a person-specific factual predicate as interchangeable. ECF 222 (filed 9 June 2025) resolves portions of a motion to dismiss; the audit above concerns the earlier status and public reason records. [Case docket](https://www.supremecourt.gov/docket/docketfiles/html/public/22-1178.html).

The public chronology documents a changed disposition despite an unavailable pair of fact-level reasons. The two administrative actors should remain separately resolved, with no automatic match between TSA's order and TSC's later status notice. Within an authenticated matched pair, an explicitly partial record permits a change to citations for a shared disclosed reason, while add/omit judgments remain unknown when the relevant earlier/later list is partial. [Executable assertions](../../tests/test_p2P27PartialReasons.mjs). This incomplete case is evidence that a public status reversal alone is an insufficient input to the proposed benchmark.

As a procedural comparator, [Kashem v. Barr, 941 F.3d 358 (9th Cir. 2019), slip pp. 9–11](https://cdn.ca9.uscourts.gov/datastore/opinions/2019/10/21/17-35634.pdf) already describes who receives review submissions, who recommends a disposition and who issues a final order. It permits restricted disclosures and describes actual dispositions in a different case. Use it to populate institutional roles, with the Fikre records establishing the case-specific facts above. A viable held-out positive case must provide an admitted factual correction, traced versions and matched official reasons with the applicable authority identified.
