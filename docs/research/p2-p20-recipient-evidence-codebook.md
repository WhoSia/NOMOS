# P2-P20 — Paired case review codebook

Unit: one named recipient, original and replacement version where relevant, one decision route, and a fixed observation window. Use pseudonymous case references. A general data-access response or an unanchored letter cannot bind a version to a later decision.

| Endpoint | Code true when | Keep unknown when |
|---|---|---|
| Revised version received | Authenticated recipient confirmation or an equivalent recipient record links receipt to the specific revised version | Issuer has only a dispatch entry, unversioned acknowledgement or historical recipient list |
| Revised version relied on | Contemporaneous decision evidence identifies that version and its substantive use | Recipient opened the report, or similarity of outcomes is the only clue |
| Reopened | A dated, substantive reconsideration started on the stated route | Intake, upload, or acknowledgement alone |
| Reversed | A decision on this reconsideration changed the stated adverse decision | A refund, generic eligibility letter or a different property is the only record |
| Usable on that route | At the point of approval or concrete offer, documented supply and a valid response window made the opportunity usable under the declared criterion | Missing availability, offer/notification, or an applicable expiry is not dated |

Require explicit evidence for a negative endpoint, such as a complete in-scope recipient log showing no receipt. Otherwise leave it unobserved. A different form of relief may still exist outside the route. A recorded reliance finding is not a but-for causal estimate.

Paired pilot: give the same source excerpts, timestamps and authority type to R1 legal/contextual/relational baseline and NOMOS adjudicators. Mask reference labels. Independently record support, refutation or underdetermination and the exact strongest locator used. Compare unsupported positive findings, informative abstentions, and verified endpoint coverage; adjudicate discordant interpretations without viewing system labels. Use a closed packet with a genuine receipt, a verified alternative-route reversal, a late unusable favorable decision, and an incomplete negative control. Do not score an unknown against an invented field label. No independent paired pilot executed in P2-P20.
