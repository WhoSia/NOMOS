# P2-P31 — Challenge, uptake and institutional reuse

[Clark & Brennan (1991), original chapter pp. 127–149](https://web.stanford.edu/~clark/1990s/Clark%2C%20H.H.%20_%20Brennan%2C%20S.E.%20_Grounding%20in%20communication_%201991.pdf) · [Drive reprint scan](https://drive.google.com/file/d/17WWIQZS0iPuL_k6ujPT-pFdKa_wx8D_W/view). The grounding criterion concerns mutually evidenced understanding sufficient for the current purpose, not bare message delivery. The presentation and acceptance phases, acknowledgments and contextually appropriate responses at pp. 129–133 are useful prior art for choosing documentary observables. A recipient can answer a challenge and reject its factual claim. The acknowledgement can document communication without establishing that the recipient adopted the correction, used the corrected version or had authority to rely on it.
