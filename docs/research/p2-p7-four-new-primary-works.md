## Precise source readings and claim limits
### Park et al. (2012)
Sections II–V build upon OPM artifact/process/agent dependencies, define provenance-based access control with **user authorization** and **action validation** rules that traverse dependency paths, including a regular-expression-dependent query; section VI demonstrates an online course grading scenario; section VII examines extension with user-declared provenance. The manuscript explicitly discusses dynamic separation of duties, workflow, origin dependence and object versions. This defeats a simplistic 'NOMOS first to use provenance of institutional action to gate authorization' claim. The demonstrated sample is a model/case study, not a verified nationwide compliance deployment.

### Martin et al. (2025)
Sections II–VI present **six factorial vignette survey conditions** (Prolific recruited in March/April 2023; 30 vignettes per respondent within a condition) varying use-purpose, actor, raw data versus inferred information, and first/third-party context. The reported Survey 1 versus Survey 2 mean vignette ratings are -23.41 versus -30.88 (authors also report t=10.22); the inference condition was judged less appropriate on average in those scenarios. They also report purpose-related vignette differences and critique claiming on-device processing or inference replaces privacy guarantees. These are **reported participants' judgments of stipulated scenarios**, not directly observed legal violation rates, field effects, causal welfare recovery or universal population preferences. Journal tables and supplementary distribution need numeric audit prior to statistical reanalysis. Study explicitly binds contextual integrity to empirical measurement and thus competes with NOMOS's proposed 'novel experiment on inference-use purpose'.

### Sultan (2022) doctoral thesis
Chs. 2–5 include purpose-property matching, legal bases, provenance collection-purpose inheritance, video semantic/nonsemantic metadata, extended AERBAC need-to-know access and a prototype. Chapter 5.3 explicitly describes **MySQL backend, policy-decision-point, front end, XACML policies**; Chapter 5.4 compares extended video-content/event authorizations and privacy-aware views. Ch. 7.1 acknowledges trusted-actor assumption and trouble enforcing purpose limitation in untrusted public domains. The work is more than a conceptual proposal: it reports an implemented prototype, but an implemented prototype is not an independently established real-world efficacy benchmark or empirical legal compliance certificate. Distinguish thesis's overall results from the 2021 published Sensors paper and the separately published data-lake chapter and Computers & Security journal article.

### Engelmann & Nissenbaum (2025) preprint v1
Section II defines 'privacy nihilism' around an unjustified inference-from-anything-to-anything (EfE) premise; §III's **conceptual overfitting** criticizes convenience-driven training constructs and weak conceptual mapping. They distinguish data selection, construction of ground truth labels and model evaluation as epistemic failure points. §IV stresses that even debunking hyperbolic AI capability does not eliminate genuine threats to data-type-focused protection; §V defends context/actor/purpose-based analysis. This is **direct prior art** on epistemic legitimacy and authority of generated person-level claims, not merely about transmission; withdraw NOMOS's assertion that existing contextual-integrity theory leaves inference warrant wholly untouched. Nonetheless, normative analysis of AI inference does not itself prove every particular institution's legal authority or person-specific outcome.

## Combined novelty reclassification
- Provenance-path action control: Park 2012 already.
- Purpose-limited video data, metadata/prototype: Sultan 2022 already.
- Purpose, actors, raw vs inferred empirical privacy judgments: Martin et al. 2025 already.
- Conceptual legitimacy of inferred traits and ground-truth construction: Engelmann/Nissenbaum 2025 already.
Therefore any new P2 theorem must have a *specific institution-claim-use authorization or remedial consequence* and demonstrate incremental testable utility over these four, the 2004/2006/2021 prior works and a strong expert legal audit. No such result currently verified. P2 research stage remains OPEN, novelty/real outcome HOLD.
