# P2-P33 — Ibrahim establishes the original error and orders a cross-record remedy

[Ibrahim v. DHS, N.D. Cal. No. 3:06-cv-00545-WHA, ECF 701-1](https://www.justsecurity.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/02/Ibrahim.Ruling.2.2014.pdf), 62 F. Supp. 3d 909. The order was dated January 14, 2014. The reproduced redacted copy bears a February 6 filing stamp, and a separately available [unsealed version bears ECF 682](https://papersplease.org/wp/wp-content/uploads/2014/04/ibrahim-order-unredacted-unsealed-15apr2014.pdf). Use the order date, docket reproduction and later compliance deadline as distinct events.

At pp. 8–9 and findings ¶¶22, 37–38, the district court finds a November 2004 form error by an FBI agent, export from a government database to the No Fly List, and a resulting refusal of boarding in January 2005. The agent conceded the erroneous selection at trial. The court also finds that TSC determined in early January 2005 that she should not have been on that list and that she was removed thereafter. This fixes a particular initial incorrect designation and a particular correction route. It does not establish that every subsequent restriction or visa decision was caused by this original designation.

Conclusion §A at p. 38 orders a search across the government's listed records and either removal of references to the erroneous designations or a same-paragraph correction directing readers to give them no reliance for any purpose. Sworn declarations were to detail the action taken for each list. The remedy expressly contains a retention-with-correction option as well as deletion. §§B–D deal separately with notice of visa ineligibility grounds, listing-status information, and notice that she could apply for a discretionary visa waiver. A waiver application opportunity is a distinct outcome from a granted waiver, visa or known later admissibility decision.

[Ibrahim v. DHS, 912 F.3d 1147 (9th Cir. 2019) (en banc)](https://cdn.ca9.uscourts.gov/datastore/opinions/2019/01/02/14-16161.pdf) recounts the conceded error and court-ordered cleanup in reviewing the fee and bad-faith issues. The [2014 implementation filings](p2-p33-ibrahim-compliance.md) supply a later, separate evidentiary step.
