# NOMOS-0.885 P2-P14 — Strong Relational Rival and a Prospective Disagreement Protocol
Date 2026-11-11. Stage P2 adversarial research, novel theory HOLD.

## Researcher-defined baseline versus proposed addition
R0 weak baseline: only whether Bob directly disclosed data. This misses third-party dependencies. R1 STRONG baseline combines Barocas & Levy's 2020 tie/similarity/difference mechanism with Viljoen's 2021 vertical/horizontal and governance-legitimacy assessment. The baseline also receives actor, context, purpose, source facts and governing law whenever available. It already flags noncontributor dependencies and population-wide legitimacy, so **NOMOS cannot count such flags as added value**. G = identical R1 + explicit institutional use of a specific proposition, legal warrant source and post-decision correction/outcome chain. G's extra fields are not a new legal theory unless they yield a correct independently adjudicated improvement over R1 + strong case-law auditing, and are also not in principle inaccessible to R1 if extended.

## Conceptual distinction
A privacy mechanism is how Bob's information can become inferable from Alice; an institutional authorization is whether actor I may issue/use claim Q at time t for purpose U; collective legitimacy is whether population-level data relations are governed fairly. These are analytically different but all have strong predecessors. The object of comparative evaluation is **adjudicated decision disagreement**, not whether the proposed framework has more columns.

## Controls
- Same input source packet, rule/authority date and time budget for R1 and G.
- Explicit unknown vs false and independent same-person/same-event/same-recipient attribution; no inventions of evidence or law.
- R1 includes purpose, relational mechanisms, group effects, governance and expert rule checks, not a strawman provenance-only baseline.
- G may only claim an increment if an independent blinded reviewer adjudicates a real R1 error that G corrects using evidence equally available to both.
- Null result: R1 and G agree or G supplies a nonunique restatement; report no original decision advantage.

## Design-only sample
Four toy cases: a family relationship disclosure, group similarity prediction, elimination in a defined candidate set, and population-level governance arrangement. In each, give R1 the original source's taxonomy and give G no privileged facts. An additional 'warrant' Boolean by itself is circular and must not be credited as empirical improvement.

## Adversarial criterion
1. Independent case and case-claim selection, freeze before scoring.
2. Two domain experts or an appropriately documented independent review process with conflict adjudication.
3. Paired confusion/abstention matrices with separate legal uncertainty.
4. Explicit analysis of whether contextual integrity, inference economy, relational governance and construct-validity measurement already identify the alleged problem.
5. Check identity/recipient source provenance to avoid data leaks or mismatched individuals.

## Conclusion
Two newly acquired manuscripts are **defeaters** of broad theory originality. This unit creates a reproducible *evaluation contract* only, not a new theorem, real-world outcome or evidence that NOMOS outperforms relational frameworks. No consent-sensitive actual personal dataset is used.


### P2-P14.6 — Domain of dependence is prior-art variable
Barocas/Levy 2020 §II makes privacy dependency type an explicit mechanism dimension. If a test varies whether Alice and Bob are related, similar in a dataset, or distinguishable by elimination, a correct strong baseline already detects those differing information-production routes. Relabeling mechanism as a new NOMOS stage supplies no incremental information.
