# P2-P32 — Fikre's notice identifies a channel

The March 2015 order is an operative TSA order. The Courtright declaration identifies a May 6, 2016 reporting chain. TSC informed government counsel of the removal and counsel told Fikre's counsel. The declaration describes the general determination that he no longer met the placement criteria. The individual deciding officer, the underlying facts and the operative removal record are unresolved. [No. 22-1178 petition, App. F ¶¶4–5 and App. G](https://www.supremecourt.gov/DocketPDF/22/22-1178/268295/20230602180319346_FBI%20v.%20Fikre%20Final.pdf#page=150).

The defendants lodged status notice ECF 98 on May 9, 2016, three days after the communication to counsel described in the declaration. The district court recounts the filing in ECF 105. The Supreme Court treats the removal as an unexplained notification and addresses mootness. [ECF 105 pp. 4, 13](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCOURTS-ord-3_13-cv-00899/pdf/USCOURTS-ord-3_13-cv-00899-3.pdf#page=4) · [601 U.S. 234, 238–239](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/23pdf/22-1178_p8k0.pdf).

After remand, the district court denied the renewed motion as to mootness while granting it on the facial claim and as to three defendants. That procedural disposition leaves the decisionmaker and version-specific reasons for the original delisting open. [ECF 222 pp. 1, 4](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCOURTS-ord-3_13-cv-00899/pdf/USCOURTS-ord-3_13-cv-00899-8.pdf).

[Kashem's administrative account](p2-p32-kashem-redress.md) distinguishes TSC's recommendation and TSA's final administrative adjudication. The actual route in Fikre remains unverified. For the pairing input, retain a status notice with an unknown deciding authority.
