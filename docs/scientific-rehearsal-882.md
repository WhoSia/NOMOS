## Mainline warning signs
- **Reanimated literal physics:** claiming F_semantic=0 as a measured biological/physical fact or person essence.
- **Refusal romanticization:** calling any unpopular assertion true or Camusian virtue merely because it dissents.
- **Outlier essentialization:** inferring an absent inner life from atypical mourning/expressive behavior.
- **Instrument drift:** celebrating a workflow, verification receipt or compliance score without returning to person attribution.
- **Evidence laundering:** independent institutions copying one erroneous source and counting the copies as independent confirmation.
- **False closure:** marking a complete repair because a ticket was closed, a debt was cancelled, or a record edited, without matching the person-specific endpoint.
- **Chronological amnesia:** reporting a new discovery without checking its direct 0.2–0.881 predecessor.
- **Over-correction:** forbidding justified predictions, narrow lawful closure, proportional act accountability or historical truth preservation.
