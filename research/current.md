# NOMOS-0.885 — Current research, P2-P36

NOMOS asks when an institution can carry an observation forward as an authorized judgment about a person, and what a later correction changes for each recipient. Retention, disclosure, present use, authority to decide and observable consequences require distinct evidence. [Current formal specification](../spec/purpose-bound-record-afterlives-885.md) · [Research genealogy](../docs/genealogy.md).

## Evidence from paired institutional acts

- [Ibrahim](../docs/research/p2-p33-ibrahim-findings.md) identifies an originating error and a correction order. The [compliance exhibits](../docs/research/p2-p33-ibrahim-compliance.md) report deletion, correction in place and notice. They also reproduce a visa refusal on April 14, 2014. The refusal followed the January order and preceded the April 15 compliance filing. The record inspected leaves the receiving official's evidentiary version and actual reliance open.
- [Patrick](../docs/research/p2-p32-patrick-opinions-readback.md) distinguishes a mortgage denial, a credit-reporting dispute response and a reported correction. The corrected version delivered to the lender and any later lender decision remain open.
- [Kashem](../docs/research/p2-p32-kashem-redress.md) separates a recommendation, final decision authority and notice. [Fikre](../docs/research/p2-p32-fikre-authority.md) supplies a communication chain but leaves the individual delisting authority open.
- [Hayden and Mischel](../docs/research/p2-p28-hayden-mischel.md) distinguish explanations for behavior that conflicts with a prior impression. The [Ko sample-flow audit](../docs/research/p2-p25-ko-study-1.md) identifies a remaining question about how the neutral group entered the analysis. Those studies inform the person-attribution question within their designs.

- [Cortez](../docs/research/p2-p36-cortez-recipient-readback.md) provides a dealership's readback after the reporting agency said the disputed material was absent. The OFAC alert still appeared. The original sale and later report check concern different events.
- [Federal Water](../docs/research/p2-p29-chenery-agency-dates.md) supplies two published agency orders and separates the 1945 order's session date, filing date and retroactive legal effect.

## P2-P37 — Evidence ceilings, not case-count expansion

The [cross-case evidence-boundary audit](../docs/research/p2-p37-evidence-boundaries.md) separates (1) correction actually made, (2) recipient version/receipt, (3) authority to use it, (4) reason-specific reliance, (5) a distinct later decision and (6) independently observed outcome. Cortez documents a statement/readback discrepancy, not an established correction-then-redecision failure. Patrick's denial predates the asserted correction. Ibrahim's visa decision and the no-fly remediation are distinct questions. Fikre's messenger is not proof of the deciding official. Reclassification of dates in Federal Water does not redate the historical act. Psychology's falsehood correction is not legally inadmissible-but-true information.

Use the same frozen packets for ordinary provenance audit, domain-specific legal review and the NOMOS protocol, with blind source-based scoring of unsupported causal transitions under equal access/time. The [ranked living missing-evidence register](../docs/research/p2-p37-missing-evidence-register.md) distinguishes held originals from still-unobserved facts. Comparative advantage, legal outcomes and real downstream effectiveness are unverified.
