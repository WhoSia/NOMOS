## Primitive question

Who may move a historically retained or compacted person-judgment branch back into active review, on what trigger, for what scope, and without allowing historical accessibility or reopening itself to restore current authority over the person?

## Reopening authority

NOMOS separates:
- authority to retain/access history;
- authority to admit a bounded reopening;
- current person-judgment authority;
- current consequence authority.

**AUTHORITY TO OPEN HISTORY IS NOT AUTHORITY TO GOVERN FROM HISTORY.**

## Reopening state machine

The stage distinguishes:
- dormant historical retention;
- petition admission;
- bounded rehydration;
- active review;
- fresh current-authority reconstitution;
- reclosure.

**REOPENING HISTORY DOES NOT REAUTHORIZE THE HISTORICAL JUDGMENT.**

## Trigger and standing

Recognized trigger classes include:
- integrity/provenance defects;
- material new evidence;
- newly discovered descendant or consequence dependency;
- activated compaction debt;
- stale-resurrection signal;
- validated rule/model/query defect;
- institutional self-correction.

Newness alone is insufficient. A trigger must be materially linked to a claim, dependency, consequence or declared reopen task.

Standing is typed across the subject/representative, materially affected third parties, repair-duty institutions, independent oversight and qualified successors.

**STANDING TO ASK FOR REOPENING IS NOT STANDING TO WIN THE REOPENED QUESTION.**

## Threshold asymmetry

The evidence required to justify review admission may be lower than the evidence required to restore adverse current authority.

**THE THRESHOLD FOR REVIEW MAY BE LOWER THAN THE THRESHOLD FOR REAUTHORIZATION.**

Adverse current authority still requires fresh current warrant and fresh adoption.

## Dual failure surface

0.853 treats two failures symmetrically but not identically:
- **permanent dormancy** — compaction, migration or institution-controlled provenance makes a material challenge practically impossible;
- **permanent reactivation** — historical availability, repeated petitions or periodic review keep old person-models permanently eligible for renewed scrutiny.

**REPEATED REOPENING MUST NOT BECOME A SUBSTITUTE FOR FRESH EVIDENCE.**

## Scope locality and privacy

A trigger normally reopens only the implicated branch/subgraph. Global reopening requires a separately justified shared generator/query/provenance/consequence defect.

Privacy-sensitive history is rehydrated only to the extent required by the admitted reopen task.

## Paper-level viability

0.853 survives initial prior-art pressure because the paper-level object is not generic contestability. It is the second-order authority problem created **after** a judgment has already been corrected, superseded or compacted:

**historical retention → reopening admission → bounded rehydration → fresh review → fresh current authority.**

The strongest publishable contribution is the formal separation of retention authority, reopening authority, current judgment authority and consequence authority together with trigger/standing/scope rules and the dual anti-dormancy / anti-reactivation failure model.

Novelty is a credible research hypothesis, not a universal priority claim; it remains subject to fuller legal/archival/contestability prior-art review before publication.
