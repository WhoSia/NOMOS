# Current Research Head — NOMOS-0.854

**Status: STRONG PASS / CLOSED**

## Primitive question

When a validated defect found in one reopened historical person-judgment branch implicates a model, query, data pipeline, rule, institutional generator, provenance chain, monitoring surface or review route shared by other retired branches, when does that defect create a duty to reopen a wider historical cohort?

## Anti-reinvention boundary

0.854 does not rediscover generic systemic error, root-cause analysis, class-wide remedies or the proposition that an individual challenge can reveal a shared defect.

Recovered predecessors:
- **0.814** — wider reopening requires a bridge through a shared component, monitoring failure or architecture rather than institutional identity;
- **0.844–0.847** — system-level repair, adoption, burden distribution and consequence authority remain distinct;
- **0.850** — correction propagation follows dependency semantics rather than raw graph reachability;
- **0.851** — governing correction state may be a frontier;
- **0.852** — retired historical branches may be compacted without retaining live authority;
- **0.853** — retention/access authority, reopen authority, current judgment authority and consequence authority are distinct.

External novelty pressure is material. Anton Abramov's 2026 preprint *When Recalculation is not a Remedy* already argues that a substantiated defect materially linked to a shared decision-generating component can move the minimum remedial inquiry to that component's operational cohort and shift scope-investigation burden toward the institution. NOMOS therefore does **not** claim novelty for that broad component-linked systemic-repair move.

The surviving 0.854 object is narrower: **historical cohort recall after branches have already been retired/compacted**, with explicit exposure-frame reconstruction, evidence-mode-specific sample-to-cohort escalation, bounded non-recall, review-capacity obligations and a firewall between cohort recall and individual merits/consequence authority.

## Core separation

**LOCAL DEFECT ≠ CLASS DEFECT.**

**SAME INSTITUTION ≠ SHARED DEFECT ROUTE.**

**SAME MODEL LABEL ≠ SAME MATERIAL EXPOSURE.**

**COHORT RECALL ≠ INDIVIDUAL MERITS REVERSAL.**

**SYSTEMIC REOPENING ≠ OLD CONSEQUENCE REAUTHORIZATION.**

## Exposure graph

Historical branches and shared components are related by material exposure, not organizational membership.

For a validated defect implicating component set C_d, the candidate recall frame is the set of retired branches that can be shown to have traversed a defect-relevant component in a way material to the disputed judgment.

**THE RECALL COHORT FOLLOWS EXPOSURE, NOT INSTITUTIONAL IDENTITY.**

Institution, department, model family or model version labels are insufficient when query, data path, consequence class or actual component exposure differ.

## Evidence modes and sample-to-cohort escalation

0.854 distinguishes:
- **mechanism-universal** — the validated defect necessarily/deterministically touches each exposed branch;
- **census** — every exposed branch is inspected;
- **probability sample** — prevalence transport requires a cohort-matched frame, outcome-independent selection and reported uncertainty;
- **stratified sample** — material exposure/consequence strata must be represented;
- **targeted diagnostic sample** — useful for mechanism discovery but not an ordinary prevalence estimate.

**A TARGETED FAILURE SAMPLE MAY LOCATE A MECHANISM WITHOUT ESTIMATING ITS COHORT PREVALENCE.**

## Recall duty and bounded non-recall

Once a defect is validated, a shared material route is established and historical exposure is reconstructible, known exposed branches cannot be silently omitted.

A non-recall decision must be bounded by a reason such as:
- lack of material dependency on the defective component;
- independent correction/re-derivation with fresh authority;
- an explicitly bounded absence of any remaining reopen/consequence task;
- documented lawful impossibility carried as repair debt rather than treated as proof of correctness.

The affected person is not required to discover every institutionally generated historical branch.

## Recall authority ceiling

Systemic reopening may authorize:
- cohort identification;
- notice and standing;
- bounded rehydration;
- prioritization/sequencing;
- case-level review;
- correction or re-derivation where individually warranted.

It does not itself authorize:
- automatic merits reversal;
- automatic restoration of an old adverse judgment;
- automatic consequence revival;
- unrelated institution-wide reopening;
- new person-side evidentiary burdens.

**COHORT RECALL OPENS REVIEW; IT DOES NOT DECIDE MERITS.**

Old consequence authority remains frozen pending fresh case-level warrant and adoption.

## Capacity and paper recall

A declaration that thousands of historical cases have been reopened is not meaningful if there is no review-capable path.

A valid recall plan therefore requires:
- notice/standing route where feasible;
- review capacity or principled sequencing;
- preservation of individual contestability;
- explicit unresolved-exposure cells rather than false completion.

## Paper-level viability

0.854 survives only in narrowed form after novelty-kill.

The publishable contribution is **not** the claim that shared defects can require systemic repair. The stronger candidate contribution is a typed theory of **historical cohort recall**:

validated local defect → defect-relevant shared component → reconstructible historical exposure frame → evidence-mode-specific cohort escalation → bounded recall → fresh case-level review.

This adds two boundaries that adjacent shared-component remedial accounts do not by themselves settle:
1. how to transport evidence from a reopened sample into a population of already retired/compacted person-judgment branches;
2. why system-level recall authority still does not settle individual merits or revive prior consequence authority.

Novelty remains a research hypothesis subject to fuller publication-grade review.
