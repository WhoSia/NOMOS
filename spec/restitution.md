# NOMOS-0.857 — Restitution Shortfalls and Residual Repair Debt

The audit distinguishes review completion, restoration, compensation, debt disposition and final closure. Repair is not a scalar: `review_complete` does not imply `restitution_complete`, and compensating one dimension does not reverse historical opportunities or the authority of still-live derived copies.

## Normative boundary
- Acknowledging a residual harm does not establish unlimited retroactive jurisdiction or a legally enforceable compensation amount.
- Reversal cannot recreate past opportunities; compensation may be appropriate without recreating the prior world.
- Third-party reliance must be reviewed without becoming evidence that the defeated judgment was true.
- Interim protection expiration does not automatically reactivate the old adverse decision.
- Discharge requires a declared typed task scope, reviewable reasons, opportunity to challenge, independent authority and a record of genuinely unresolved losses.
- Full substantive justice is not established by structural audit PASS.

## Anti-reinvention
Seth Lazar (2008), *Corrective Justice and the Possibility of Rectification*, distinguishes harm correction from wrong correction and explicitly denies that monetary compensation restores the complete status quo ante. *After Harm* (2025) argues decision reversal or decommission alone can leave algorithmic imprints. NOMOS novelty, if any, is in typed closure certificates for correction of retired person-judgments rather than the general insight that full rectification is impossible.

## Implementation
`analyze_restitution(packet)` checks RD001–RD033 and outputs pass/fail plus reason receipts. It is an audit of supplied declarations, not an economic harm estimator or a legally operative remedy engine.

## Known limitations
Packet fields are assertions; the checker cannot authenticate payments, reconstruct opportunity counterfactuals, enforce court judgments, or measure non-financial harm. Separate external evidence and case-level authority are necessary.
