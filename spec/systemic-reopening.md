# Systemic Reopening Constitution — NOMOS-0.854

## Primitive question

When a validated defect found in one reopened historical person-judgment branch implicates a model, query, data pipeline, institutional generator, rule, provenance chain, monitoring surface or review route shared by other retired branches, when does that defect create a duty to reopen a wider historical cohort?

## Anti-reinvention boundary

0.854 does not rediscover class actions, ordinary root-cause analysis, algorithm audits, or the generic proposition that one successful challenge can reveal a system-level problem.

Recovered NOMOS prior art already establishes:
- 0.814: one post-recovery failure can justify wider reopening only through a shared-component or monitoring bridge;
- 0.844–0.847: system-level repair, adoption authority and distributional burdens are distinct;
- 0.850: correction propagation follows dependency semantics rather than graph reachability alone;
- 0.851: correction state can be a multi-head frontier;
- 0.852: retired history remains reconstructible without retaining live authority;
- 0.853: historical access, reopen admission, current judgment authority and consequence authority are distinct.

External novelty pressure is especially strong from Anton Abramov's 2026 preprint *When Recalculation is not a Remedy*, which argues that a substantiated defect linked to a shared decision-generating component can shift the burden of investigating remedial scope to the institution. NOMOS therefore does not claim novelty for that broad move.

The narrower 0.854 object is **historical cohort recall after branch retirement/compaction**, including exposure-frame reconstruction, sample-to-cohort escalation, bounded non-recall, and a firewall between cohort recall and individual merits/consequence authority.

## Core separations

**LOCAL DEFECT ≠ CLASS DEFECT.**

**SAME INSTITUTION ≠ SHARED DEFECT ROUTE.**

**SAME MODEL LABEL ≠ SAME MATERIAL EXPOSURE.**

**COHORT RECALL ≠ INDIVIDUAL MERITS REVERSAL.**

**SYSTEMIC REOPENING ≠ OLD CONSEQUENCE REAUTHORIZATION.**

## Shared-defect bridge

A local defect may support wider historical reopening only when the proposed cohort is connected to the defect through a material shared dependency such as:
- the same defective query or selection rule;
- the same model component actually used in the relevant decision path;
- the same data transformation or provenance defect;
- the same generator or template capable of reproducing the defeated representation;
- the same review/monitoring route whose failure is itself validated;
- a common rule or threshold mechanism.

Brand, institution, department, model family or version labels are not enough without exposure evidence.

## Exposure graph

Let historical branches be B and shared components be C. Define an exposure relation E ⊆ B × C.

For validated defect d implicating component set C_d, the initial recall frame is not all branches in the institution. It is the subset:

R_d = { b ∈ B : ∃ c ∈ C_d such that (b,c) ∈ E and the exposure is material to the disputed judgment }.

This is an inquiry frame, not a merits verdict.

## Sample-to-cohort escalation

0.854 separates five evidence modes:
1. mechanism-universal: a validated defect necessarily/deterministically affected every branch traversing the component;
2. census: every exposed branch is inspected;
3. probability sample: selection supports prevalence transport to a defined cohort;
4. stratified sample: transport is conditional on coverage of material exposure/consequence strata;
5. targeted diagnostic sample: useful for mechanism discovery but not an ordinary prevalence estimate.

A targeted failure sample cannot be laundered into a population prevalence claim.

Probability-based escalation requires:
- a cohort-matched sampling frame;
- selection independent of the observed error outcome for ordinary prevalence claims;
- uncertainty reporting;
- material-stratum coverage where stratification is used.

## Recall duty

Once a defect is validated, a material shared route is established, and historical exposure is reconstructible, known exposed branches cannot be silently omitted. Non-recall requires a bounded reason, such as:
- branch not materially dependent on the defective component;
- independently corrected/rederived branch with fresh authority;
- no remaining consequence or reopen task under an explicitly bounded constitution;
- lawful impossibility documented as a repair debt rather than treated as proof of correctness.

The person should not bear the burden of discovering every historical branch produced by an institution-controlled shared mechanism.

## Recall authority ceiling

Systemic recall may authorize:
- branch identification;
- notice;
- bounded rehydration;
- prioritization/sequencing;
- review of affected historical person-judgments;
- correction/re-derivation where individually warranted.

It does not itself authorize:
- automatic merits reversal;
- automatic restoration of an old adverse judgment;
- new person-side evidentiary burdens;
- institution-wide reopening outside the exposure bridge;
- indefinite reactivation of unrelated history.

During recall, stale/old consequence authority should remain frozen unless a fresh case-level warrant and adoption independently justify current action.

## Capacity and notice

Class-wide reopening is not meaningful if the institution declares thousands of cases 'reopened' without a review-capable path. A recall plan therefore requires:
- affected-person or representative notice/standing route where feasible;
- review capacity or principled sequencing;
- preservation of case-level contestability;
- explicit handling of unresolved exposure cells.

## Constitutional rule

A validated local defect creates a wider historical-reopening duty only to the extent that other retired branches can be materially connected to the defective shared mechanism or process. The recall cohort follows reconstructible exposure, not institutional identity. Sample evidence can widen the inquiry only under an evidence mode whose transport assumptions match the claim. Cohort recall reopens review; it does not decide individual merits or revive old consequence authority.
