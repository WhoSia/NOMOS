## Return to NOMOS's origin
The original material-opportunity / normative-evaluation distinction survives the rejection of literal social-physical forces. A person's apparent choice or compliance is jointly conditioned by available opportunities and the institution's evaluative framework. See docs/origin.md and docs/genealogy.md. The number denotes a point in a continuous inquiry, NOT an independent completed theory.

## Primitive and nonentailments
A provisionally defensible rule can distribute substantial burdens differently across people with different resources, dependencies and exit alternatives. Conversely unequal outcomes may reflect justified relevant differences. Hence neither rule defensibility nor surface uniformity certifies the full burden allocation; inequality alone does not establish unfairness.

Keep five typed objects separate:
- R: the rule and its stated aim, including validity conditions;
- X_i: claimant-relative exposure and feasible alternatives;
- E: warrant for claimed burdens and effects;
- J: who owes which reasons to whom and can access the evidence needed;
- M: institutional authority to respond, adapt and repair.

## Counterworlds
- Same rule, different feasible options for two people; identical recorded compliance fails to identify consent or equal burden.
- Different burdens justified by a relevant difference; unequal exposure need not be arbitrary.
- Institution holds population-wide data while requiring each affected person to prove an unavailable common mechanism; epistemic asymmetry matters for allocating investigation duties.
- A single adverse decision is corrected yet shared decision-generating components continue to affect others; repair scope not inferred from one case.
- An affected party's account is not heard though aggregate institutional reports are abundant; frequency and volume do not substitute for standing.

### Updated inference contract
The burden profile is **typed and situated**: learning costs, compliance costs and psychological costs, with cumulative history and the feasible opportunity set as further axes. They have no empirically established common unit, so an additive universal scalar is prohibited under NOMOS's rejected 0.1 social-physics analogy. Even the underlying papers share a genealogy and may summarize the same studies: **three publications do not automatically count as three independent causal replications**.
Institution-only cohort evidence can trigger the question of a bounded duty to investigate when a claimant produces an adequately grounded case-level allegation; it does **not** entail a universal burden shift, access to all sensitive third-party records, a valid legal claim or an authorized remedy. Distinguish individual testimony, source availability, targeted production, investigation, ultimate merits, and downstream response. A reduced application task could still generate system-wide delays if agency capacity is inadequate.
