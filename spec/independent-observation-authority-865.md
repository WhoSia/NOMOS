## P1 — The shared-root observer attack
Two observer IDs may differ while both rely on the same operator-controlled database, signed gateway, common administrator, or institutionally incentivized report. Identity inequality is not independence. A narrow negative decision rule: for any apparent readback pair, if the observer evidence paths share an untrusted controlling root, evidence independence is **NOT_ESTABLISHED**; absence of known collision is also not positive certification. An attested root is itself a claim requiring independent examination.

2. **Authority:** provenance chain beyond observer label; no common untrusted root to the subject of the audit; noncollusion and capture cannot be assumed.
3. **Semantics:** source defeat, fresh warrant and generator replay are distinct tests (0.850), including identical-output fresh warrant.
