# NOMOS-0.858 — Residual Repair-Debt Succession

## Explanandum
Who is competent and obligated to carry remaining restitution duties when the original decision-maker, record holder and review body are no longer identical—or the original institution has ceased to exist?

## Typed boundaries
Record custody ≠ operational succession ≠ review power ≠ legal restitution liability. Institutional dissolution does not automatically extinguish the repair question; operational or archival succession does not automatically establish restitution liability.

## Four duty states
1. Scoped obligation assigned to a legally warranted successor.
2. Orphan HOLD with accessible records and a real escalation/adjudication route.
3. Independently warranted, task-relative discharge.
4. Invalid disappearance: neither successor nor accessible escalation nor warranted discharge.

## Anti-reinvention
Public-records succession, agency succession, public-law reorganization and restitution doctrines already exist. South Korean Public Records Management Act Article 25 specifically separates closed institutions with or without successors and regulates records transfer. These rules warrant archival continuity, NOT universal liability of the custodian. NOMOS's narrower candidate is a task-typed, provenance-preserving handoff of already identified person-judgment repair debts, with both under-transfer and over-transfer falsifiers.

## Hard cells
- Original agency abolished, records go to national archives but no agency inherits review power; HOLD pending competent legal authority, no fictional archive liability.
- Institution renamed or merged; predecessor judgment cannot become magically new or epistemically valid.
- Contractor hosted system; vendor possession of weights or dataset does not itself transfer remedial liability.
- Successor possesses files but statute excludes compensation power.
- Claimant cannot access records, while successor says the duty is time-barred: record access and legal merits must be separately examined.
- Recipient scoring system remains active after predecessor dissolves.
- Temporary adverse protection lapses during reorganization.
- Multiple successor bodies split archive, review and funding duties; typed assignments cannot be compressed into a single institution label.

## Execution
`analyze_successor_duties` is a 32-guard structural negative audit. It does not verify the actual legal authority of any entity: `authority_evidence:true` is only a claim. Real deployment requires jurisdiction-specific statutes, transition instruments and independent review.

## Normative ceiling
Orphan HOLD is not an enforceable award; archive custody is not liability; human claimants must not lose practical recourse solely because the originating institution vanished.
