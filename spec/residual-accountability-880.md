## P0 typed claims: four non-substitutable evidence channels
L(t,d) = operative/legal status of decision d after a later Act and any qualifying exception; H(t_0,d) = dated historical compliance and notice record at time of action; Q(p,t) = identifiable, legally available and practically reachable review/compensation route for person p; Y(p,t) = actual person-linked restoration supported by independent readback. Additional X(frame,t) = screened population exposure with frame and denominator.
L is no proof of H, Q or Y; X alone cannot estimate attrition to Q or Y. The opposite is also important: H describing failure cannot resolve modern legal merits, and Q accessibility cannot certify completed compensation. No universal inference that courts disregard savings statutes or that CDDA automatically compensates affected parties.

## P1 falsification worlds
W1: Identical current validity and case facts, but A's contemporary institution logged timely legal review, while B recorded a five-year delay after identified legal concern. Same L, different historical H. No legal-penalty conclusion follows without a separate authority.
W2: Identical screening estimate X, but cohort A has independently verified notifications and receipt counts, cohort B has only unverified aggregate projections. Same X, no common Q/Y measure.
W3: Two people have a validly advertised CDDA claim route; one has a fully authenticated application and independent payment readback, the other has no recorded application. Same nominal Q, different evidence of actual Y; neither legal entitlement is inferred.
W4: Later Act specifies validity only 'merely because' one defect; independent defect claims remain an open legal question. The abstract does not adjudicate them.
W5: Senate Community Affairs supports enactment while Scrutiny of Bills Committee records a separate concern about possible extinguishment of lawful entitlements. Passage does not magically turn either report into evidence of actual payments/harms.
W6: Publication of claimant route by agency alone cannot show notices reached the historically exposed frame; suppressed contact records make attribution of 'zero applications' underidentified.
Required counterfactuals: with/without notification, with/without law change, and independently verified pre/post individual consequences. Avoid adversarially withholding legitimate notice to manufacture identification.
