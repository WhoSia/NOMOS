## P0-R3 stronger paired countermodels

Fix observable nonconformity = true. Consider four possible worlds: (A) inward affect exists but social display differs; (B) affect differs as well; (C) conscious dissent from the social display norm; (D) conscious dissent accompanied by a separately established wrongful act and a validly authorized bounded act-level consequence. These worlds share a behavioral observation but disagree about experienced affect, deliberate refusal and actual conduct. Consequently nonconformity by itself neither establishes inner absence nor excuses an independent wrong.

Proposed **minimal separation**, not a universal legal rule:

- Appearance or refusal is observed conduct.
- Experience, intention and disposition are separate hypotheses requiring appropriate warrants.
- A separately established act plus legitimate bounded authority may support task-specific accountability.
- No one source of nonconformity and no merely duplicated copies of it license a person-wide essence claim.
- The interpreter's assertion that an unusual person is an ontological singularity is itself subject to the same act-to-person evidential limits.

This construction supplies a counterexample to both *every nonconforming person lacks moral feeling* and *every nonconforming person is virtuous or innocent*. It deliberately does not prove a law of human psychology, a total prohibition on case-specific person-related evidence, Camus's authorial intent or a real judicial defect.
