# NOMOS-0.855 — Recall-Capacity Triage
## Primitive
A valid historical recall cohort requires a separately authorized ordering policy when review capacity is scarce.
**QUEUE PRIORITY IS REPAIR-SEQUENCING AUTHORITY, NOT PERSON-JUDGMENT AUTHORITY.**

## Anti-reinvention
Queue fairness, scheduling efficiency, backlogs, prioritization and algorithmic ranking are established areas. NOMOS contributes only if it preserves a *post-error-repair* authority boundary: an institution's review order must not become a new judgment about deservingness, character, guilt or old consequence authority.

## Four nonimplications
Recall eligibility does not imply queue priority.
Queue priority does not imply substantive merits.
Waiting does not equal acquiescence or a valid adverse judgment.
Finishing earlier does not confer greater personal worth or evidentiary authority.

## Valid triage parameters
Independent repair-relevant considerations may include: ongoing consequences actually imposed, reversibility of delay, time-sensitive rights or access windows, reliable case-specific deadlines, institutional responsibility for prolonged delay, and accessible challenge mechanisms. None is automatically a total ordering.

## Hard cells
- FCFS can perpetuate unequal access to notice.
- Cheapest-case-first may maximize throughput while deferring severe ongoing harms.
- Severe-consequence-first is legitimate only where severity is established independently of the defeated person label.
- Random lottery can break permissible ties but cannot evade non-negotiable obligations.
- Capacity exhaustion cannot silently convert unresolved recalls into permanent denials.
- Priority changes require reasons, a contest route and periodic revision.
- Emergency departures need separate authorization, sunset and logs (0.848).

## Development
`analyze_recall_triage` is a structural audit, not a scheduler, judge, or recommender.
QT001–QT029 identify omissions, overreach, access bias, unreviewed delay and authority laundering.
Run: `PYTHONPATH=src python -m nomos examples/recall_triage_safe.json --audit-recall-triage`

## Research limits
Formal proof that a queue is substantively fair cannot be inferred from these boolean guards. Calibration needs service-time distributions, consequence data, deadlines, counterfactual policies, uncertainty, intersectional access and empirical independent review. PASS certifies only absence of encoded violations.

## Next hypothesis
Backlog repair may require an independently justified temporary consequence suspension when review is delayed; such suspension must not itself adjudicate underlying merits.
