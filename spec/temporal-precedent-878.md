## The P0 research contract
Use different time axes: time information was received; time rule was operative; time decision issued; time appeal lodged/stay obtained; time legal interpretation became authoritative in a particular system; time a new law took effect (including any *actually enacted* retroactive effect); time of downstream correction. Do not equate a later judicial interpretation with a new statutory amendment. Distinguish contemporaneous reasonableness and validity under governing law, actual institutional actions, available legal remedies, and observed consequences.
Synthetic rivals:
A: Agency appeals adverse interpretation, obtains court-ordered stay with stated scope and review timetable; legal status and permitted actions differ from appeal without a stay.
B: Agency appeals but does not obtain a stay; administrative delay and ignoring operative orders may impose an independent burden despite agency confidence.
C: Higher court changes the legal interpretation; open future cases, live appeals, time-barred finalized cases and retrospective statute each need separately justified routes. No universal auto-reopen.
D: Underlying legislation remains in force but an agency knowingly administers a nonconforming rule while hoping for later legislation to validate it. A legislative proposal is not enacted retroactive authority.
E: An interim pause protects one population but delays lawfully payable benefits for another; distributional consequences and third-party reliance require independent inquiry.
Historical mechanism proof, individual relief and policy-change causal attribution remain open. No new automatic legal engine is called for merely to enumerate states.
