## Existing doctrine adopted
- US FRE 609(c): certain pardoned, annulled or rehabilitated convictions are not admissible to attack witness truthfulness under specified conditions. It does not erase history or bar every other lawful use. https://www.law.cornell.edu/rules/fre/rule_609
- California DOJ automatic criminal record relief: qualifying records may be marked and access/dissemination restricted depending on recipient and purpose, without deleting all source records or revoking every authorized access. https://oag.ca.gov/fingerprints/record-review and https://oag.ca.gov/careers/record-relief 
- US FRE 105/106, McKeon and Salerno in the predecessor 0.884 define separate courtroom evidentiary problems and do not become universal administrative obligations.

## Rival court / negative controls
A. **Permitted narrow reuse:** retained source record used for a legally authorized, properly bounded purpose, with no imaginary general ban.
B. **Stale descendant:** source status annotated/corrected, but later decision still relies on an obsolete derivative; receipt and actual decision change separately unverified.
C. **Dependent copies:** several copied records are treated as multiple independent confirmations; preserve dependence edges (Greenberg 2009 is a scientific-citation analogy, not direct case-law authority).
D. **Reclassification overreach:** partial relief misconstrued as total erasure or universal denial of access.
E. **Correction-performance inflation:** record label updated yet no verified material change in actual downstream consequence.
F. **Historical repair paradox:** retaining accurate audit history need not confer continuing current governing authority.

## P0-R4 — Adverse decision disclosure and practical correction (2026-10-17)
Verified source contrast. California Penal Code 11105(t) requires an authorized recipient of DOJ criminal-history information, when it is a basis for an adverse employment, licensing or certification decision, to expeditiously furnish the affected applicant a copy; this is NOT an automatic reversal requirement (2025 text: https://law.justia.com/codes/california/code-pen/part-4/title-1/chapter-1/article-3/section-11105/ ). For employment background reports obtained from consumer reporting agencies, FCRA imposes a *pre-adverse-action* report and rights-summary disclosure, and an adverse-action notice after a decision with consumer reporting company and dispute information (FTC: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/background-checks-what-employers-need-know ). These sources govern different triggers and channels; do not treat FCRA as automatically governing every DOJ fingerprint check or every license decision.

Reasoning and controls: source availability (R), adverse basis disclosed (B), timely notice (N), effective capacity to dispute (C), correction of source (S), delivery to descendant (D), authorized recipient re-evaluation (E), and observed outcome (O) are separate observables, not an invented universal eight-step legal doctrine. Negative control: copy or notice without accessible record correction cannot prove procedural redress; another negative control: source corrected but downstream recipient continues to rely on stale data, requiring case-level proof before asserting violation. Positive control: accurately disclosed, legally relevant information may support the decision without an obligatory favorable outcome, and a correct adverse decision may survive a fair contest. NOMOS 0.261, 0.822, 0.850, 0.864–0.865 precede this decomposition. Harvest provenance protocol requires independent documentary evidence for each claimed transition and particular outcome; agency guidance is not evidence of recipient compliance. ## P0-R6 — Rhodes: independent reporting accuracy versus dispute-triggered reinvestigation (2026-10-17)
Primary 11th Circuit opinion *Rhodes v. First Advantage Background Services Corp.*, 24-11005 (Oct. 30 2024), https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/ca11/24-11005/24-11005-2024-10-30.html . The opinion distinguishes FCRA §1681e(b), reasonable pre-report procedures assuring maximum possible accuracy, from §1681i(a), post-notice reasonable reinvestigation of the *specific disputed item*. The duplicate Record 2 was alleged to correspond to no real proceeding and accuracy/reporting claims survived pleadings; specific duplication was not disputed before litigation, so §1681i reinvestigation duty as to duplicate was not triggered. Court did not conclusively establish liability, revised rental status, damages or final downstream remedy. Source statute §1681i https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title15/html/USCODE-2024-title15-chap41-subchapIII-sec1681i.htm .

This dual-duty structure provides a positive control against saying all correction duties arise solely after the affected individual detects and describes every error. It also rebuts the opposite universal claim that any generic dispute forces investigation of all report entries. ## P0-R7 — Duty separation, procedural effect, and a retirement guard
FCRA 15 USC 1681e(b) requires reasonable procedures assuring maximum possible accuracy when preparing a report, including before any particular complaint (https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2024-title15/html/USCODE-2024-title15-chap41-subchapIII-sec1681e.htm). FCRA 15 USC 1681i(a) requires reasonable reinvestigation for information the consumer actually disputes and notifies the consumer reporting agency about (https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/15/1681i). ## P0-R8 — Dispute scope and surviving duties: adversarial test
**Separate legal obligations by trigger.** FCRA §1681e(b) accuracy procedures are not conditioned on a particular consumer dispute, while §1681i(a) reinvestigation is triggered by actual notice of disputed information; Rhodes (11th Cir 2024) did not reach a final factual finding that all duplicate reports violate the Act. Four rivals: (1) no dispute yet fictitious duplicate report: assess pre-report accuracy; (2) specific notified duplicate dispute: assess reasonable reinvestigation; (3) notified dispute of underlying case disposition only: cannot automatically infer notice about duplicate docket; (4) accurate and statutorily permissible entry: fair challenge need not change decision. Never conflate statutory availability, merits judgment, and actual consumer outcome. This is inherited from NOMOS 0.261, 0.752, 0.822, 0.850, 0.865, and original opinion https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/ca11/24-11005/24-11005-2024-10-30.html .
