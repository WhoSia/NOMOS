## Existing doctrine adopted
- US FRE 609(c): certain pardoned, annulled or rehabilitated convictions are not admissible to attack witness truthfulness under specified conditions. It does not erase history or bar every other lawful use. https://www.law.cornell.edu/rules/fre/rule_609
- California DOJ automatic criminal record relief: qualifying records may be marked and access/dissemination restricted depending on recipient and purpose, without deleting all source records or revoking every authorized access. https://oag.ca.gov/fingerprints/record-review and https://oag.ca.gov/careers/record-relief 
- US FRE 105/106, McKeon and Salerno in the predecessor 0.884 define separate courtroom evidentiary problems and do not become universal administrative obligations.

## Rival court / negative controls
A. **Permitted narrow reuse:** retained source record used for a legally authorized, properly bounded purpose, with no imaginary general ban.
B. **Stale descendant:** source status annotated/corrected, but later decision still relies on an obsolete derivative; receipt and actual decision change separately unverified.
C. **Dependent copies:** several copied records are treated as multiple independent confirmations; preserve dependence edges (Greenberg 2009 is a scientific-citation analogy, not direct case-law authority).
D. **Reclassification overreach:** partial relief misconstrued as total erasure or universal denial of access.
E. **Correction-performance inflation:** record label updated yet no verified material change in actual downstream consequence.
F. **Historical repair paradox:** retaining accurate audit history need not confer continuing current governing authority.

## P0-R1 — California DOJ automatic relief: notification is not demonstrated compliance (2026-10-17)
Original agency guidance https://oag.ca.gov/fingerprints/automatic-record-relief-penal-code-sections-851.93-and-1203.425 and FAQ https://oag.ca.gov/node/596528 ; statute https://law.justia.com/codes/california/2024/code-pen/part-2/title-8/chapter-1/section-1203-425/ . Distinguish DOJ statewide record/relief annotation, electronic court notification, the court's separate case management and statutory non-disclosure duties, exceptional authorized dissemination under PC11105, employers' purpose-specific consideration and actual independently observed decisions. Relief annotation does not remove all historical records. Positive control: retained criminal-justice access when expressly authorized. Opposite-direction control: when source databases lack material disposition information and relief appears wrongly granted, DOJ instructs comparison, data correction and re-evaluation; correction is not synonym for universal suppression. These are documented pathways, NOT evidence that a particular court failed, employer misused information, or actual life result changed. NOMOS 0.822 descendant coverage and 0.850 receiving operation already address the conceptual distinction; 0.852 distinguishes retained from governing use.
