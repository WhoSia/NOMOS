## Insufficiency witness
Two possible people may share exactly the same observable unusual mourning behavior, yet differ in inner grief, interpersonal care and independent criminal conduct. Therefore there is no universally sound total function `nonstandard mourning display -> no love -> bad character -> guilt of unrelated act`. This *does not* establish that affect is irrelevant in every scoped inquiry or that mourning customs impose no interpersonal obligations. Social codes can matter to how people experience care, and a bounded affective expression can be probative when it is supported by independent case-specific evidence. Social ritual appraisal cannot substitute for adjudicating actual grave wrongdoing.
