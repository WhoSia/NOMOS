## P0-R1 — McKeon primary opinion and explicit boundaries (2026-10-17)
Source: United States v. McKeon, 738 F.2d 26 (2d Cir. 1984), https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/F2/738/26/135199/ . Court rejects categorical exclusion of a lawyer's earlier opening but imposes stringent conditions for later *evidentiary* use: clear inconsistency of factual claims, not merely speculation or arguments; a sufficient basis to treat the earlier proposition as the client's testimonial equivalent, not agency alone; and meaningful handling of competing explanations, with concerns about privilege, strategy and counsel disqualification. The distinction between a binding judicial admission within a case and a prior evidentiary admission is explicit in the opinion. This is existing Second Circuit doctrine, not a general person-character law or a Korean rule.
Internal reuse: 0.246 authorized delegation does not license arbitrary new scope; 0.261 correction-burden lens reveals costs of contest; 0.724–0.798 copied enduring predicates; 0.881 bounded closure; Greenberg 2009 warns apparent corroboration may share origins. Positive witness: a well-grounded clear factual inconsistency can be properly considered when safeguards hold. Negative witness: generic counsel speculation is not automatically the defendant's factual assertion. ## P1-R2 — Purpose-relative admissibility and downstream re-use (2026-10-17)
**Verified prior doctrine; no novelty claim:** FRE 401 makes relevance proposition/issue-relative; FRE 105 expressly contemplates evidence admissible for one party or purpose but not another, and a limiting instruction on timely request; FRE 403 permits exclusion when probative value is substantially outweighed by listed dangers; FRE 404 restricts some propensity uses while leaving other permissible uses and exceptions. Sources: https://www.law.cornell.edu/rules/fre/rule_401 ; https://www.law.cornell.edu/rules/fre/rule_105 ; https://www.law.cornell.edu/rules/fre/rule_403 ; https://www.law.cornell.edu/rules/fre/rule_404 . These rules govern appropriate federal evidentiary contexts, not every administrative or non-US tribunal.

P1-R2 counterexamples: A previously admissible factual statement remains useful for a properly framed disputed fact with lawful admission and meaningful response; its inclusion is not a license to infer stable character for a new unrelated purpose. A source copy with a limiting purpose label dropped must not be treated as independent confirmation. A successful source correction does not establish whether a later receiving decision was actually changed. ## P1-R3 — FRE 105 and amended 106: use limitation versus completeness (2026-10-17)
Source-law adoption: FRE 105 protects limits on use against particular parties or for particular purposes, requiring proper-scope limiting instruction on timely request (https://www.law.cornell.edu/rules/fre/rule_105). FRE 106, effective amended December 1 2023, now covers statements generally rather than only writing/recorded statements and permits fairness-required completing statements over a hearsay objection (https://www.law.cornell.edu/rules/fre/rule_106 ; https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/28a/courtrules-Evid/article-I). The completing statement must actually be required by fairness: this is NOT unconditional full-record disclosure. Rule 105 does not guarantee that limiting instructions eliminate all risk of prejudice. These are distinct US federal evidentiary doctrines, not a universal administrative duty.

### Rival tests
(1) A partial factual quotation that misleadingly omits a qualification may justify contemporaneous completion under rule 106; the qualification's inclusion does not automatically make the original proposition inadmissible for all purposes. (2) Evidence admitted for narrow question A must not silently gain unrestricted authority for unrelated question B; rule 105 illustrates purpose-specific admissibility. (3) Complete source context alone cannot establish the institutional power to make a new person-wide attribution: NOMOS 0.752, 0.836, 0.837 and 0.850 remain distinct constraints. (4) A useful bounded quotation not misleading without the missing section remains a positive control: no duty to append every irrelevant sentence. The project contribution is a cross-institution transfer audit proposal, NOT a claim that rules 105/106 directly govern non-federal decisions.

## P1-R5 — Historical retention vs current use authority; new independent successor candidate (2026-10-17)
Primary legal comparators: FRE 609(c) restricts use of certain pardoned, annulled or rehabilitated convictions for *witness impeachment* on specified findings and conditions (https://www.law.cornell.edu/rules/fre/rule_609). It is not general historical erasure or categorical inadmissibility for all contexts. California DOJ's automatic record relief uses a notation and purpose/recipient-specific dissemination limits without itself erasing the underlying record (https://oag.ca.gov/node/596528). DOJ further distinguishes sealing, automatic relief, expungement and statutory destruction and preserves some criminal justice access (https://oag.ca.gov/fingerprints/sealing-orders). These are genuinely existing, non-identical regimes, not an empirical certificate that all recipients actually complied.
