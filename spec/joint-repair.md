# NOMOS-0.859 — Fragmented Successor Jurisdiction and Joint Repair

## Primitive
Local agency compliance does not certify end-to-end correction when archives, review authority, enforcement infrastructure, recipient records, and restitution budgets are distributed.

## Independent constraints
- Joint repair requires an end-to-end task graph, known organizational capabilities, authorized handoffs, privacy constraints and an accessible challenge route.
- A coordinator has workflow authority but does not inherit universal merits, consequence or compensation jurisdiction.
- A local institution's PASS cannot discharge a chain of unresolved recipient dependencies, missing permissions or undelivered funds.
- A legal veto must remain reviewable; genuine jurisdictional limits cannot be bypassed by creating fictional superior coordinating authority.
- Attribution of joint liability is not automatic even when coordinated action is required.

## Negative audit
JC001–JC031: explicit task dependencies, participating institutions, lawful data passage, single accessible procedural route, third-party/recipient obligations, interim safeguards, residual ledger and independent end-to-end receipts.

## Predecessors
NOMOS-0.814 topology, 0.834 shared causation, 0.839 review independence, 0.841 routing power, 0.849 institutional succession, 0.853–0.858 reopen/recall/priority/protection/debt/succession.

## Method ceiling
`joint_repair.py` checks asserted booleans and set inclusion, not real jurisdiction, laws, data or actual relief. Broader executable integration should replace untrusted self-attested authority flags with authenticated evidence.
