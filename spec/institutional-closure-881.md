## Primitive and precise scope
When an institution controls who is notified, permitted to apply, and observed, under what conditions may it certify that **a named person's specific process or outcome is closed** on the basis of records it generated? The epistemic existence of a receipt, lawful procedural finality, causal person-level benefit, and legitimate later **reuse of a person-judgment** are separate authority claims. A bounded verified examination or legally effective waiver may close its named task without certifying health recovery, compensation received, recovery of lost opportunities, or the character of an individual. Do not deny valid narrow closure merely because comprehensive repair remains uncertain.
