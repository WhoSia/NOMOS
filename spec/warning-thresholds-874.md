## Core primitive
At time t, distinguish the accessible information I_t; strength, specificity and credibility of signals E_t; foreseeable harm and its latency H_t; institution's lawful intervention set A_t; costs of erroneous intervention C_t; reversibility V_t; feasible independent inquiry and decision timeline T_t; actual effect Y_t; and standing to demand explanations. An action may be "investigate", "preserve evidence", "pause specified decisions", "independently review", "continue with safeguards", or "suspend a process", depending on lawful authority and documented options.
Two false implications: (a) incomplete evidence does not always justify inaction; (b) a credible warning does not always authorize wholesale suspension. Distinguish protective investigation thresholds, targeted suspension thresholds, final attribution standards, and retrospective responsibility judgments. Do not treat these as one universal numerical cutoff.
## Four competing counterworlds
A. Credible high-severity signal; reversible temporary pause feasible and cheap. Continued exposure during avoidable delay has its own justificatory burden.
B. Weak or ambiguous signal; suspension creates serious interruption for legitimate beneficiaries. Time-limited independent inquiry and reversible safeguards may be more defensible.
C. Warning is strong but institution lacks legal power to suspend independently; record, refer, escalate and preserve evidence rather than infer unlimited authority from potential harm.
D. A formal suspension occurs but records or downstream actions continue causing effects; intervention decision and verified consequence cessation are separate, continuing NOMOS-0.864–0.865.
## Counterfactual and temporal test
Compare the response actually selected at time t to feasible lawful alternatives under the information reasonably accessible at t, not with perfect hindsight from a later commission. Document warning provenance and timing, options, anticipated costs, safeguards, affected-party testimony and downstream effects. A plausible alternative may be authorized but unavailable in practice. Keep uncertainty and competing explanations explicit. No universal moral or legal scalar.

## P1 — Intervention reversal, precedent attack, and temporal review (2026-10-09)
### Novelty breaker
The European Commission's 2 February 2000 Communication on the Precautionary Principle (COM(2000) 1 final) already requires proportionate, non-discriminatory, consistent, reviewable measures, attention to costs of action and inaction, and risk-sensitive alternatives to blanket prohibition. https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=COM%3A2000%3A0001%3AFIN . Therefore the generality of proportional reversible intervention is NOT a NOMOS discovery. Its potential original contribution is time-indexed institution-held warnings and institutional capacity, differential recipient opportunity sets, feasible protective measures, review standing, and downstream verification as jointly traceable but non-scalar objects.
### Preference reversal witnesses (synthetic, not historical fact)
A: Strong credible warning; prospective harmful automated collection; lawful temporary halt on collection while independent review continues. Targeted reversible relief may outrank both total shutdown and passive continuation.
B: Weak, highly ambiguous warning; lawful services would be disrupted by blanket suspension; bounded independent sampling/audit and evidence retention may outrank a whole-system halt.
C: Strong warning that a specific debt is being disputed but narrow stay is feasible; narrower relief dominates in its affected-person dimension even when program-wide legal validity remains unresolved. Commission recommendation 18.1 suggests withholding debt recovery during dispute subject to express legal authority, but is 2023 reform guidance and not retrospective binding law.
D: Mid-inquiry evidence changes, or temporary protection causes newly observed adverse consequences; a measure reasonable at t0 may require revision at t1. A nominal sunset without active review does not establish renewed legitimacy.
### P1 proposition and limits
There is no context-free linear order among investigate, narrow pause, broader pause, and continue-with-safeguards, because changing the uncertainty set, lawful authority, reversibility, harm exposure and affected-party opportunity sets can reverse their ordering. This is a conceptual counterworld result, not a measured risk threshold nor binding universal legal test. Do not infer individual knowledge from a later commission report. Distinguish first authorization, continuation, renewal, and repeal of temporary measures, and ask who can trigger independent review.
