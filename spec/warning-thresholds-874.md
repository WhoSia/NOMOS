# NOMOS-0.874 — Warning Thresholds, Proportionate Intervention & the Time of Institutional Responsibility: Evidential Escalation, Reversible Precaution, Correction Delay & the Primitive Question of When an Institution Must Act Before Its Knowledge Is Complete
## Official opening — 2026-10-09
The user fixed the title above. NOMOS-0.874 is the migration from 0.873's question "which institutional actors could know and intervene" to "at what evidential stage must an institution select a proportionate response". NOMOS-0.873 and earlier inquiries remain historically live. This opening is theory P0, not a declaration of verified empirical thresholds or legal rules.
## Core primitive
At time t, distinguish the accessible information I_t; strength, specificity and credibility of signals E_t; foreseeable harm and its latency H_t; institution's lawful intervention set A_t; costs of erroneous intervention C_t; reversibility V_t; feasible independent inquiry and decision timeline T_t; actual effect Y_t; and standing to demand explanations. An action may be "investigate", "preserve evidence", "pause specified decisions", "independently review", "continue with safeguards", or "suspend a process", depending on lawful authority and documented options.
Two false implications: (a) incomplete evidence does not always justify inaction; (b) a credible warning does not always authorize wholesale suspension. Distinguish protective investigation thresholds, targeted suspension thresholds, final attribution standards, and retrospective responsibility judgments. Do not treat these as one universal numerical cutoff.
## Four competing counterworlds
A. Credible high-severity signal; reversible temporary pause feasible and cheap. Continued exposure during avoidable delay has its own justificatory burden.
B. Weak or ambiguous signal; suspension creates serious interruption for legitimate beneficiaries. Time-limited independent inquiry and reversible safeguards may be more defensible.
C. Warning is strong but institution lacks legal power to suspend independently; record, refer, escalate and preserve evidence rather than infer unlimited authority from potential harm.
D. A formal suspension occurs but records or downstream actions continue causing effects; intervention decision and verified consequence cessation are separate, continuing NOMOS-0.864–0.865.
## Counterfactual and temporal test
Compare the response actually selected at time t to feasible lawful alternatives under the information reasonably accessible at t, not with perfect hindsight from a later commission. Document warning provenance and timing, options, anticipated costs, safeguards, affected-party testimony and downstream effects. A plausible alternative may be authorized but unavailable in practice. Keep uncertainty and competing explanations explicit. No universal moral or legal scalar.
