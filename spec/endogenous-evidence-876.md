# NOMOS-0.876 — Endogenous Evidence, Review-Access Asymmetry & the Self-Sealing Provisional Measure: Selective Observability, Suppressed Counterevidence, Renewal Feedback Loops & the Primitive Question of Who Can Contest an Intervention That Controls the Evidence of Its Own Necessity

## P0 — Endogenous evidence and renewal access
The intervention may change who can supply the evidence used to assess its renewal. Analyze latent states, observation, effective standing, independent review authority and downstream correction. An absence of appeals under curtailed access is not independent evidence that a measure is necessary.

Status: OPEN. Prior NOMOS 0.340, 0.842–0.845, 0.856, 0.875 and selective-label literature prohibit claims of novelty for generic selection feedback.

## P1 — Minimal observational-equivalence attack (2026-10-10)
Define a target cohort and legally relevant binary state Z (which must be separately specified for the claim). Let A be a restrictive policy, O=1 if independently verifiable evidence about Z enters the review record, and H=(A,O,OZ) the recorded observation. Suppose A=1 makes O=0 for all members of a stratum. World W0 has Z=0 for that stratum; W1 has Z=1, with the same population, policy and observation process. Then H is identical in W0 and W1. A renewal decision that depends only on H therefore cannot distinguish these worlds. If the appropriateness of renewal differs between them under a separately stated criterion, it is not identifiable from these logs alone. This is a toy impossibility under zero review support, not a new theorem about all institutions.
If A merely reduces the probability of O, the result depends on overlap, sampling assumptions and measurement quality; strict impossibility does not automatically follow. Further, an independent audit can recover facts yet fail to make a remedy effective when the reviewer has no authority to alter decisions. Information access, procedural standing, authoritative review and observed repair are different conditions.
## P1 — Rival worlds and testable interfaces
A. The restriction prevents affected recipients from producing corroborating material; renewal cites a low challenge rate. Test actual eligible population and opportunity to submit, not merely the administrative count.
B. The low challenge rate reflects few well-founded objections and genuine accessible review. External random or other representative inspection could support that explanation.
C. Appeals are submitted but selectively recorded, categorized or escalated; evidence production and official observation diverge.
D. A third-party advocate can document missing cases, but official reviewers lack independence or authority to change the policy. Observability may be improved without restoration.
E. Independent review is effective for current appeals but hidden downstream records continue affecting people; appeal success is not evidence of complete repair.

## P2 — What actually breaks the evidential self-seal (2026-10-10)
**Audit-sampling conditional lemma (existing survey-sampling mathematics):** On a fixed, enumerated target population of N cases with well-defined binary, verifiably assessable Z_i, an independent audit samples each i with known inclusion probability pi_i>0. If inclusion is design-controlled, observation accurately measures Z_i and audit participation does not change the target Z_i, then the Horvitz–Thompson form (1/N) sum_i O_i Z_i/pi_i is design-unbiased for the finite-population fraction (1/N) sum_i Z_i. This is a sufficient recovery condition for THAT factual prevalence under specified design; it neither adjudicates an unaudited individual's merits nor identifies what would have happened without the policy. No new statistical theorem is claimed.
**Observation ≠ remedy gate:** (G1) a defensible population frame and observation coverage; (G2) actual practical ability for affected parties or qualified independent witnesses to present contrary evidence; (G3) legally competent, meaningfully independent review able to change the result; (G4) verification that decisions and downstream effects were actually corrected. Evidence-gathering may recover G1 without G2–G4, and nominal appeals may grant G2 without G1 or G3. These are separate authority questions; no numerical morality score.
**Adversarial countermodel:** a representative shadow audit discovers an erroneous restriction, but its findings cannot be admitted by the legally competent reviewer. Statistically identified errors coexist with practically impossible contestation. Conversely accessible appeal procedures with no independent observation of people barred from submissions cannot demonstrate policy accuracy by low appeal counts.
**Temporal challenge:** at renewal t+1, preserve whether observation channels are caused by the intervention R_t, not only contemporaneous appeal rates; assess changes in denominator, access pathways, admissibility rules, and reviewer capacity. A case-level successful appeal cannot certify that all cohort-level selection has been fixed. This is an integration proposal with older NOMOS 0.843 shadow-audit and 0.864 effect-repair ancestry, not proof of new primitive laws.
**Empirical falsification:** inspect actual appeal attempt/submit/reject pathways, case inclusion frame, independent sample overlap, decision-change authority, and downstream impacts. Find a source-grounded case with the alleged loop, and show whether external evidence changes renewal decisions; otherwise keep self-sealing as a potential mechanism, not a finding about Robodebt.
